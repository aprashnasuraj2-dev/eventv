What is actually known

The date is calendar-derived. The retail event is decentralized.

There is no single Black Friday organizer that sets universal store hours, discount depth, country availability or return rules. Retailers can run promotions before, on or after November 26, and “Black Friday” branding can cover a longer period.

Decision rule / before any purchase

Discount percentage is not the same thing as value.

FTC shopping guidance recommends comparing the total cost, including shipping, handling, delivery, taxes and other fees, and checking the deal terms, product condition, seller, return policy and delivery promise. The FTC’s deceptive-pricing guidance also explains why a “former price” should be a genuine basis for a comparison rather than an artificial reference point.

Reference-price problem“40% off” is arithmetic against a claimed comparison price. It does not independently prove the item usually sold at that price.
Total-cost problemA cheaper sticker price can lose after shipping, mandatory membership, fees, taxes or a costly return path.
Variant problemModel suffix, storage, screen, included accessories, warranty, condition and seller can make two offers non-comparable.
Timing problemA product can be discounted before or after Nov 26. “Black Friday price” does not prove “best price of the year.”

Counterexample: Offer A is advertised “35% off” at $649 from a $999 reference price. If your dated, same-model evidence shows the product sold for $679 recently, the practical reduction versus your observed baseline is only about 4.4%—before shipping or fees.

Policy check / before checkout

A cheaper price can be a worse purchase if the return path is expensive.

FTC online-shopping guidance recommends checking shipping, delivery, refund and return terms before buying. Sale and clearance items can have different return rules. The FTC also notes that if a seller does not state a shipping time, federal rules generally require shipment within 30 days after receiving the buyer’s information/payment authorization.

International readers

November 26 is the U.S.-calendar anchor, not a global retail rule.

United States

Black Friday is conventionally tied to the day after U.S. Thanksgiving. Store hours, online launch times and return rules remain retailer-specific.

United Kingdom / Europe

Retailers may use Black Friday branding, but U.S. Thanksgiving is not a local public holiday. VAT, consumer rights, currency and promotion timing are jurisdiction-specific.

Canada

Canadian retailers may participate, but shipping origin, duties, taxes, warranty and return logistics can change the true cost of a cross-border offer.

Australia

Promotions may follow the same marketing date while operating in a very different local timezone. Never convert a U.S. retailer launch time unless that retailer publishes a timezone.

FAQ / no invented deals

Black Friday 2027 questions answered with the uncertainty intact

When is Black Friday 2027?

The conventional Black Friday date is Friday, November 26, 2027. OPM lists U.S. Thanksgiving Day as Thursday, November 25, and Black Friday is the following day. This is a calendar derivation, not a universal retailer promotion schedule.

What time does Black Friday 2027 start?

There is no universal start time. Each retailer can publish its own online and store schedules, potentially with different timezones and early-access windows. No retailer-specific 2027 start time is verified on this page.

What are the best Black Friday 2027 deals?

Unknown this far in advance. A useful “best” claim needs current comparable prices, exact model/variant information, total checkout cost, availability and seller terms. EventV does not invent future prices.

Does 50% off mean a product is half its normal market price?

Not necessarily. It means the current price is mathematically 50% below the stated reference price. Whether that reference price is a meaningful recent selling basis requires evidence. Compare your own dated observations and the exact product variant.

Should I wait for Cyber Monday?

No universal answer exists. Future price and stock are unknown. If waiting is acceptable, record the current comparable offer and set a personal threshold; do not assume Cyber Monday will be cheaper.

Can a return policy differ for sale merchandise?

Yes. FTC shopping guidance specifically recommends checking refund and return rules for sale items because sellers can apply different policies, including return shipping or restocking conditions.

Freshness / future update policy

What must be reverified when 2027 retailer campaigns appear

Retailer sale window

Record exact date, clock, timezone and whether it applies online, in-store, members or all customers.

Product identity

Record model number, variant, condition, bundle contents, seller and warranty source.

Price evidence

Separate retailer reference price, your dated observed prices, item price, shipping, taxes/fees and membership cost.

Availability

Stock, backorder, shipping estimate and pickup eligibility can change after the price is captured.

Return / price match

Record sale-item exclusions, return window, return shipping, restocking fees and price-adjustment rules.

Corrections

If a retailer changes the deal, preserve the old timestamp and explain the change instead of silently overwriting history.
