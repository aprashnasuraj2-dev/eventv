Charity route: keep fundraising and personal costs separate

The organiser describes limited charity-team entries subject to each official charity’s capacity and terms. Its application page states a fundraising minimum of $2,500 at the time checked; individual teams may have higher requirements. It lists different entry fees for US and non-US residents. Verify the actual current charity agreement, fundraising responsibility, fees, deadline and availability before committing.

The pledge gap is an amount still to raise under your input; it is not automatically the amount you personally owe. Whether you are responsible for a shortfall depends on your written charity terms. Personal expenses here exclude the fundraising pledge and can include fees, travel and other expenses you enter. A zero fee is a placeholder, not a claim that entry is free.

Why the charity deadline needs verification

The organiser’s application page describes charity entries as first come, first served until 10 September 2027 or the charity’s capacity is reached, whichever is earlier. Its application page labels that date Friday, while its event FAQ labels the same numeric date Tuesday. A calendar calculation identifies 10 September 2027 as Friday, but that does not establish whether the organiser intended the same policy deadline in every context.

Contact the organiser and the specific official charity for the current closing date, time zone, remaining places and payment and fundraising terms. Capacity can close a team before any published final deadline. Do not assume that a calendar calculation confirms availability or overrides the charity’s written conditions.

The organiser says only charities in its official programme have access to guaranteed charity entries. A charity fundraising application, an organiser registration and an assigned bib are distinct steps. Check what happens if the application is not accepted, the fundraising target is not reached, an injury prevents travel or the event changes. Different entry routes can have different cancellation rules.

Other entry paths require their own proof

The organiser describes time qualifier, legacy finisher, official charity, official tour operator, Chicago Distance Series, selected cancelled-entry routes and other programmes. A person should not assume one path’s deadline, fees, eligibility or cancellation policy applies to another. The ordinary application window for guaranteed and non-guaranteed routes is reported closed as of the source check; confirm the organiser’s latest update.

For the legacy finisher pathway, the organiser identifies specific Chicago race years and includes a special rule for some 2020 registered participants. A finish at a different city marathon, or a year in which someone registered but did not finish, is not automatically equivalent. Read the exact current rule rather than counting every marathon toward the Chicago legacy requirement.

For tour operators, the organiser says only official programme operators have guaranteed entry access and not every country has an approved operator. Packages may combine entry, lodging and travel differently. Ask for a written itemized quote, current availability, refund and cancellation terms, and confirmation of the operator’s official status. A travel purchase or inquiry is not the same as an accepted race registration.

The organiser’s FAQ describes cancellation rights and exceptions, including exclusions for some charity, tour and deferred entries. It also says event fees may be non-refundable and that a future application and fee can be required. Do not apply those general statements to a particular individual without reading the current route-specific terms.

Race-weekend and spectator planning

The organiser’s participant information describes packet pickup and identification requirements; confirm current opening times, location and ID rules directly. A travel plan should allow for packet pickup, the actual assigned start time, venue entry and any accessibility arrangements. This page does not provide a live start list, weather forecast or race-day operations feed.

For spectators, the organiser’s information describes a course through Chicago neighborhoods and a planned official race app. Those statements do not guarantee live tracking, network availability, exact arrival times, road crossings or access to every station on race day. Check official course maps and closures when published and choose a meeting point on the correct side of the course.
