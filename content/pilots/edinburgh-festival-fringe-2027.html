What is confirmed — and what isn't

CONFIRMED: The Edinburgh Festival Fringe Society lists 6–30 August 2027. The festival takes place across many city venues, so venue transfers matter.

PENDING: Do not treat any show entered below as an official 2027 listing. Programme publication, performance times, ticket prices, individual access facilities and booking availability must be verified with the organiser and venue. Personal “booked” status is your own note, not ticket validation.

HISTORICAL: Published 2026 box-office hours and accessibility-performance lists are not 2027 commitments. COUNTEREXAMPLE: Two performances can avoid direct overlap yet still be impossible to attend if the first ends 10 minutes before the second and venue travel requires 25 minutes.

How the conflict check works

Shows are ordered by their local start time. Each show's end is start plus your runtime. Its required clearance adds your transfer/access buffer. If the next show starts before that clearance, the planner flags the number of minutes short. Overlapping performances are identified separately from transfer-only conflicts. Longer performances may cross midnight; dates remain explicit.
