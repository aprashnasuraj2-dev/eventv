Before relying on a result

  1. Get the exact first show's finish time, not just its listed start.
  2. Account for accessibility, exit queues, weather and venue changes before booking tight transfers.
  3. Verify the second venue's late-entry policy and keep a backup show.

Counterexample: Two venues a short distance apart may still be infeasible between performances because of delayed exits or a venue's no-late-entry rule.
