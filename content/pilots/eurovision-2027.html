The planning model

Do not collapse Eurovision into one final-night timestamp.

The official ticket plan covers nine shows: three televised Live Shows plus two Preview Shows before each live broadcast. The live dates and broadcast clocks are known. Exact Preview Show date/time slots, ticket categories, prices and accessibility seat details are still pending.

21:00 CEST ≠ 21:00 BurgasBulgaria is on EEST in May, so the arena-local clock is 22:00.
Confirmed broadcaster ≠ confirmed artist/songEach broadcaster selects its entry on its own timetable.
Current participant count ≠ final countSpain and Slovenia have until December to confirm their 2027 decisions.
Ticket sale planned ≠ tickets availableOfficial Eurovision says Burgas 2027 tickets are not yet on sale.

Counterexample: a fan who sees “21:00” and assumes that means 9pm in Burgas arrives one hour late. Eurovision publishes the broadcast reference as CEST; the venue is in the EEST timezone.

Current participant state / Oct 28 snapshot

36 broadcasters are confirmed today; the field can still change.

The EBU’s October 28 list contains 36 confirmed broadcasters. Canada debuts and North Macedonia returns. Spain and Slovenia are not in the current confirmed list and have until December to decide. On today’s structure, 31 entries would be split across the two Semi-Finals, with Bulgaria, France, Germany, Italy and the United Kingdom pre-qualified for the Grand Final.

Burgas travel / verified host-city baseline

Use the host-city transport plan as it becomes operational—not generic map estimates.

The official Burgas guide says Burgas Airport is roughly 10 minutes by car from Arena Burgas and about 15 minutes from the city centre. Bus 15 links the airport with South Bus Station and the centre. Direct 2027 flight schedules are still pending, and Eurovision-specific shuttles, extended public transport, a dedicated mobility app and transport coordination are planned.

Eurovision 2027 Burgas questions

When is Eurovision 2027?

The Live Shows are Tuesday May 11, Thursday May 13 and Saturday May 15, 2027 at Arena Burgas.

What time does Eurovision 2027 start?

All three Live Shows are currently listed at 21:00 CEST. In Burgas, Bulgaria, that corresponds to 22:00 EEST in May.

When do Eurovision 2027 tickets go on sale?

The EBU says early 2027. Tickets are not yet on sale as of November 6, 2026. A free Eurofan account is required to enter the sale.

How many ticketed shows are there?

Nine: three televised Live Shows plus two Preview Shows before each Live Show. Exact preview-show times, ticket categories and prices are still pending.

How many countries are participating?

There are 36 confirmed broadcasters in the October 28 snapshot. Spain and Slovenia are still undecided until December, so the final field can change.

Who is in which Semi-Final?

Not yet decided. The semifinal allocation draw is scheduled for January 2027.

Who will qualify or win?

Unknown. Ten songs will qualify from each Semi-Final under the current announced structure, but no qualifier or winner is predicted here.

Can I watch Eurovision 2027 on YouTube?

Eurovision says the official YouTube channel will stream the Contest subject to territory and streaming arrangements. Check the official watch page for your country near showtime.
