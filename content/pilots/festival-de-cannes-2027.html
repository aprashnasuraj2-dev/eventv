Common questions

Can anyone buy a screening ticket?

Do not assume that. The organizer describes a reservation process tied to accreditation for its ticket office. A separate 2026 catalogue described limited public routes for a different programme; check each 2027 programme’s current conditions.

Does accreditation include a seat?

Not necessarily. Access rights vary and a reservation may be required and subject to availability. Confirm your specific category and the screening instructions directly.

What time does my screening start?

The 2027 programme and screening times have not been established here. The planner uses your own entries and does not display a live programme.

How much is accreditation or lodging?

We do not assert a 2027 fee or live price. Confirm any charges, cancellation conditions, taxes and availability from the seller before paying.
