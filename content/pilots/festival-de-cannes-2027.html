Common questions

Can anyone buy a screening ticket?

Do not assume that. The organizer describes a reservation process tied to accreditation for its ticket office. A separate 2026 catalogue described limited public routes for a different programme; check each 2027 programme’s current conditions.

Does accreditation include a seat?

Not necessarily. Access rights vary and a reservation may be required and subject to availability. Confirm your specific category and the screening instructions directly.

What time does my screening start?

The 2027 programme and screening times have not been established here. The planner uses your own entries and does not display a live programme.

How much is accreditation or lodging?

We do not assert a 2027 fee or live price. Confirm any charges, cancellation conditions, taxes and availability from the seller before paying.

Make an access plan in three separate stages

1. Identify the organiser and the screening

The Festival de Cannes, the Marché du Film and parallel sections have different purposes and can publish separate participation information. Start with the official event or screening page, then confirm the organiser, edition, venue, access channel and date. A calendar entry created in this guide is only your own note. It cannot verify that a film is selected, that a screening exists, or that tickets are available.

The Festival’s ticket-office page describes reservations for accredited participants and some parallel sections, subject to availability. The official 2026 catalogue also described a conditional general-public route for Cannes Cinéphiles. Those examples show why the question “Can I buy one Cannes ticket for everything?” has no reliable single yes-or-no answer. They do not establish 2027 eligibility for any particular person or screening.

2. Check your route to accreditation

The organiser has described several routes, including professional, press, cinephile and a limited programme for younger film enthusiasts. The available accreditation page still contains dates and rules for the 2026 edition. Identify the 2027 edition’s current eligibility, documentation, fees and deadline directly before applying; never assume that applying means acceptance. An official competition offered a 2027 accreditation to its winner, but that individual prize is not proof of open applications or general eligibility for every prospective visitor.

Accreditation may define access by category, venue or section. A public-access programme may have its own operator and conditions. If an organiser cannot confirm that your route covers your chosen screening, record it as unknown instead of treating a category name as a promise of entry.

3. Confirm the actual reservation

Once a screening is officially listed, check whether your particular credential can request a seat, whether reservations have opened, and whether a ticket or confirmation has actually been issued. The organiser describes availability limits and last-minute access; a previous edition’s queue outcome is not a forecast of admission. Save the organiser’s written confirmation and follow the current arrival window, accessibility and identification instructions.

Prices, additional contributions, payment rules and cancellation terms vary by product and year. A general statement that certain screenings have free tickets is not proof that travel, registration, third-party packages or other screenings cost nothing. This page does not calculate official fees or sell admission.

A realistic day: compare time, not only film lengths

For each tentative screening, identify a confirmed or estimated start and runtime, the cinema and whether there may be an introduction or post-screening discussion. Add a transfer allowance after that screening. The calculator compares the previous end time plus that allowance to the next start, even when a film ends after midnight. It uses the information you provide and cannot know whether a venue is operating, whether doors close early, or whether your ticket permits entry.

Example: a hypothetical 10:00 screening lasting 100 minutes ends at 11:40. If you estimate a 25-minute transfer and check-in allowance, the next event should begin no earlier than 12:05 under those assumptions. A 12:00 start has a five-minute shortfall; a 12:05 start is only an arithmetic fit. It is still possible to miss the screening if the first film overruns, the queue is longer or the venue requires an earlier arrival.

For a screening at another venue, estimate the whole path: leaving the room, collecting belongings, reaching the correct entrance, transport, security and any separate ticket scan. The venue label in the tool does not calculate a route or a real travel time. Two events at the same venue can still require a buffer; two venues with similar names may not be the same building.

Times are entered as Cannes local wall-clock times. If you receive an invitation in another zone, check the exact date and time zone with the sender before copying it. The app does not convert time zones, predict transport conditions, or validate a meeting invitation. Dates outside the announced 11–22 May festival window are rejected; this does not mean every proposed event inside that window exists.

Accessibility and travel: questions to ask early

The Festival’s accessibility information describes an annual guide to accessible venues and facilities. Ask the specific venue and organiser about the entrance, step-free route, accessible seating, lift capacity, hearing support, companion arrangements and arrival procedure for your screening. Confirm the edition and the type of ticket, since a general statement about a building does not guarantee that a particular seat or route meets your needs.

The Festival’s visitor welcome information describes mobility assistance and other support, while the admission page contains transport details for the 2026 edition. Check current 2027 arrangements directly: neither a previous year’s bus pass nor its dates automatically apply in 2027. A local journey may take longer during busy periods, so test your itinerary with both a normal and a more conservative transfer estimate.

If you are travelling internationally, consult the relevant French and Schengen official authorities for your citizenship, residence, travel purpose and dates. Festival accreditation does not itself grant a visa, entry to France, work authorisation or a guaranteed border outcome. The organiser may provide documentation, but a letter is not an immigration decision.

What should be in your cost estimate?

The budget model accepts one consistent currency and includes accommodation as nights multiplied by a per-night amount, one return-travel figure, a daily spending estimate multiplied by days, and other costs. Decide before entering values whether the numbers describe a single traveller or your whole group. If you enter a room’s full price as a per-person cost and multiply it elsewhere for a group, you may double count it.

Add any quoted taxes, booking or transaction fees, local transport, meals, insurance and cancellation costs when you can verify them. Rates can be refundable or non-refundable, and a quoted starting price may exclude the room or date you actually need. The tool does not perform foreign-exchange conversion or obtain a live quote. A zero in an input is simply your assumption; it does not mean that a price is known to be zero.

Compare the cost with and without refundable accommodation and a longer transfer buffer. That comparison can be useful even before the programme is released: it shows how much of your budget is already committed while the likelihood of receiving a screening reservation remains unknown. No result produced by the planner is an official ticket offer.

Keep an evidence trail without sharing personal details

  1. Record the official page, the year it concerns and when you checked it. Do not rely on a search snippet if the underlying page has changed.
  2. Mark each item as confirmed, pending, historical or your own assumption. For example, the event window is official; your screening time is a scenario until a specific programme confirms it.
  3. Recheck the screening operator, venue, admission conditions, cancellation policy and accessibility arrangements before making a payment.
  4. Export your itinerary as a local JSON file if you need a copy. The export contains the labels, venues, dates and times you entered; keep it private if those reveal personal travel plans.
  5. Remove outdated entries when the official programme changes. Browser storage can be cleared by your browser or may be unavailable; the tool is not a backup service.

Do not enter passport numbers, payment-card details, medical information, accreditation credentials or other sensitive data. The itinerary is intended for planning information only. Using the tool does not contact the Festival or reserve a seat.
