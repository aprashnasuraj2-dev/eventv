    Practical field guide

    Evidence before commitment

    Field guide 01

    Dates and status

    The tournament dates are confirmed, but a future date is not a guarantee of travel feasibility, seat availability or a broadcast subscription. Official announcements may be amended. Always check the organizer shortly before committing to nonrefundable arrangements.

    Field guide 02

    Brazil group-stage city anchors

    FIFA's August 2026 schedule announcement identifies Brazil in Rio de Janeiro on 24 June, São Paulo on 29 June and Brasília on 4 July. Those city/date anchors are different from asserting a named opponent, final kickoff time or ticket category.

    Field guide 03

    Host-city planning

    Brazil spans a large geography. A pair of matches separated by one day can be impossible to attend even when the kickoff gap looks sufficient. Airports, hotel location, venue access, weather, traffic and personal mobility can dominate the schedule.

    Field guide 04

    Travel model, not booking engine

    The itinerary calculator compares two user-entered Brazil-local match times with your own first-match duration, total door-to-door transport, early-arrival and risk allowances. It does not search flights, reserve seats or calculate real road distances. The result is a conditional planning screen, not a feasibility guarantee.

    Field guide 05

    Ticket decisions

    Use FIFA's official ticketing channels and the applicable current terms. Do not assume a registration of interest reserves inventory. Compare category, accessibility, refund/transfer conditions, payment currency and permitted resale before purchase.

    Field guide 06

    Visa and entry differences

    Entry eligibility is nationality-, passport- and purpose-dependent and may change. Check Brazil's official consular or immigration sources for your own circumstances. The page does not infer an entry permit from the tournament or a ticket.

    Field guide 07

    Accessibility

    Accessibility arrangements vary by venue and ticket category. Verify official accessible seating, companion rules, transport and stadium approach routes directly. A city appearing in the host list is not evidence of an accessible itinerary.

    Field guide 08

    Viewing rights

    Country-specific broadcast licensing matters. A 2023 broadcaster or a foreign-language stream is not evidence that the same provider can legally show the 2027 event in your country.

    Field guide 09

    Evidence discipline

    Each private note is explicitly user-supplied. A CONFIRMED label requires a HTTPS source and checked date, but the application cannot independently authenticate the source. Verify the document before relying on the claim.

    Field guide 10

    Data and privacy

    Planner entries are stored only in this browser using localStorage. Clearing site data removes them; another browser or device will not synchronize them. Exported CSV may contain personal notes; treat the file accordingly.

    Frequently asked questions

    Decisions that need a precise answer

    Are all 64 match opponents known?

    No. A published match-slot schedule and host-city assignments do not establish every final national-team pairing. Qualification, draw and knockout progression remain separate evidence steps.

    Is a ticket-interest registration a ticket?

    No. Registration does not guarantee an offer, a purchase window, an assigned seat or availability. Consult the official ticket terms.

    Does the kickoff converter show official kickoff times?

    No. You type a hypothetical or independently verified Brazil-local date and clock time. The converter only translates that entered instant; it does not assert the input is an official kickoff.

    Why might the time differ on another device?

    The browser's IANA timezone database may differ across operating systems, especially if Brazil changes future daylight-saving rules. Recheck official kickoff details and timezone legislation closer to travel.

    Can I rely on a green itinerary result?

    No. Green only means your user-entered time allowances fit the arithmetic gap. It does not confirm flight availability, check-in cutoffs, stadium entry or realistic door-to-door transit.

    What if two matches are in the same city?

    You still need local transport and venue-entry time. A same-city itinerary can fail if roads close, transport is delayed or the first match finishes late.

    Are the listed stadiums guaranteed for every match?

    The host venues are FIFA's announced venues; each specific match allocation should be checked against the latest official schedule and ticket documents.

    Which country broadcaster will show matches?

    This hub does not claim a verified broadcaster by territory. Consult current FIFA and broadcaster announcements for your country and date.

    Is Brazil visa-free for all fans?

    No. Rules depend on nationality and other factors. Tournament attendance alone is not a universal visa waiver.

    Is my notebook uploaded?

    No. It stays in your browser's local storage and can be exported or reset. This page does not provide cloud sync.

    Can I use the planner without accepting cookies?

    The core calculations work without storage. Notebook persistence uses browser localStorage, which may be unavailable in private or restricted modes.

    What does CONFIRMED mean for my own note?

    It means you entered a source URL and check date; it is not an independent editorial approval or FIFA endorsement. Cross-check every saved note.
