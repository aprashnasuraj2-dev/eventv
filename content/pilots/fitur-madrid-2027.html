Read the five-day schedule

IFEMA lists 20–22 January as professional days, 10:00–19:00. On Saturday 23 January it lists professional and public access until 20:00, and on Sunday 24 January until 18:00. These are published general hours; a particular conference, meeting or hall can have its own schedule. IFEMA’s 2027 exhibitor guide lists Hall 12, the Knowledge Hub, only on 20–22 January. Do not assume every hall is open throughout the public weekend.

The organiser’s FAQ says weekdays are reserved for accredited professionals and the general public can attend at the weekend with the applicable ticket. A professional selecting a weekday in the tool is not automatically accredited; the tool checks the broad category only. Verify registration requirements, payment, entry and any visitor age conditions directly.

For example, a public visitor planning 10:00–18:30 on Sunday receives an out-of-hours warning because the listed Sunday close is 18:00. The same interval fits the listed Saturday hours. A public visitor selecting Friday is warned about the professional-day distinction even if the times themselves fit.

A source discrepancy worth preserving

IFEMA’s edition-specific 2027 launch announcement names Puerto Rico as Partner Country. Its general FAQ currently answers a guest-country question with Mexico. A generic FAQ can be stale, or “guest” and “partner” could be used for different designations; the retrieved pages do not explain the difference. Check an updated edition-specific IFEMA source before treating either statement as the complete 2027 picture.

Similarly, the FAQ’s visitor counts describe the previous edition and the 2027 launch article gives historical figures for 2026. Neither is a measured 2027 attendance result. Exhibitor lists and programme entries can change before the fair.

Plan for the right audience

A professional visitor may be arranging meetings with tourism boards, operators or technology suppliers. A public visitor may instead compare destinations and consumer offers. Those are different jobs and can involve different pass conditions. An invitation code, registration, payment and confirmed pass are separate steps; consult IFEMA’s current process.

Before purchasing a travel product from an exhibitor, verify what is included, total price, refund and cancellation conditions, travel dates and the identity of the provider. A fair booth or promotional claim does not by itself guarantee a booking or a service outcome.

  1. Check the official date-specific programme and whether your pass covers a session or special area.
  2. Allow for queues, hall transfers, security and the shorter Sunday hours. The form does not calculate travel between halls.
  3. Verify current IFEMA transport, accessibility and companion arrangements for your needs.
  4. For international travel, consult the responsible Spanish and Schengen authorities for your own entry requirements.

The visit form operates locally and does not register you, check eligibility or purchase a pass. Do not enter names, account or payment details.
