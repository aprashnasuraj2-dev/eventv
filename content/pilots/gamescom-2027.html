FAQ

Gamescom 2027 questions

When is Gamescom 2027?

Gamescom week runs August 23–29, 2027 in Cologne. General public opening is August 26–29.

When is Gamescom Opening Night Live 2027?

The official site currently says Opening Night Live is on the evening of August 24, 2027. This page does not assert an exact show time because it has not been verified in the sources reviewed here.

What are the Gamescom 2027 public opening hours?

Private visitors: Thursday Aug 26 and Friday Aug 27 from 10:00–20:00; Saturday Aug 28 and Sunday Aug 29 from 09:00–20:00. Limited Wildcard entry on Business & Media Day, Wednesday Aug 25, is currently listed from 13:00–19:00.

Are Gamescom 2027 ticket prices available?

The current official ticket page is promoting notification for the opening of the 2027 ticket shop. This guide does not publish a price until the organizer’s 2027 admission prices are verified.

Where is Gamescom held?

At Koelnmesse in Cologne, Germany. Koelnmesse’s official address is Messeplatz 1, 50679 Cologne.

Which train station is closest to Gamescom?

Gamescom’s current arrival guidance highlights Köln Messe/Deutz, with the halls within walking distance via the South Entrance.

Which entrance is best for wheelchair users?

Gamescom’s current accessibility guidance recommends Entrance North for wheelchair users; when arriving by public transport, it recommends Entrance South or East. Some parts of the venue are not fully accessible, so re-check the 2027 hall plan and accessibility page before travel.

Is the current Gamescom hall map already for 2027?

No. The official hall-plan page currently labels the displayed allocation as 2026. This guide does not reuse 2026 hall positions as a 2027 floor plan.

Is August 25 open to normal visitors?

August 25 is Business & Media Day. The organizer says it may grant a limited number of private visitors access to the entertainment area from 13:00 with a Wildcard.

Why does this page avoid a fixed gamescom dev end date?

The current official Gamescom pages are inconsistent: visitor/home pages describe gamescom dev as Aug 23–24, while the trade-visitor page currently says Aug 23–25. Check the dedicated dev site before making a dev-specific booking.
