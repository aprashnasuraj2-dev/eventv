Understand the original general-admission payment

For one original-sale general-admission order of up to six ticket holders, the organiser’s published figures were £100 deposit plus £308 balance per person, with £11.25 postage per order. Add your own other group costs. This is historical arithmetic; it does not book an order or establish future resale pricing, deposits or stock.

Postage is applied once to the order in this model, not once per person. It models general admission only; coach fares, coach administration, parking, accessibility products and other products have different conditions and are not automatically included.

Original sale, balance and possible resale are different stages

The organiser’s September 2026 ticket announcement lists general admission at £408 per person, including a £5 booking fee. It describes a £100 per-person deposit during the original sale and a remaining £308 per person, plus postage and packing, during the specified balance period. It also says up to six tickets could be booked per transaction. Those amounts and rules describe the original product and its published process.

The announcement now says tickets sold out and there will be a resale of cancelled or returned tickets in spring 2027. It does not give a precise resale date in the statement retrieved for this guide or guarantee how many tickets will be returned. Check the official festival and authorised ticketing pages for the current registration, sale, price and payment rules when resale information is actually announced.

Do not assume an unsold or returned ticket is reserved for you because a resale is planned. A registration, a queue position, an application, a payment attempt and a confirmed purchase are different states. The form above performs arithmetic on the published original-sale figures; it cannot check your registration, enter a sale or determine whether a ticket is available.

The organiser identifies See Tickets as the authorised festival ticketing channel and says tickets are personalised and non-transferable. A third party offering to transfer a ticket, use your registration or sell access outside the official process may not provide a valid ticket. The organiser warns not to share registration or payment information with people claiming to obtain tickets for a fee. Verify the current rules from the source rather than relying on a resale advertisement.

Work through the arithmetic once

For two hypothetical ticket holders in a single original general-admission order, the listed deposits total £200. The listed balances total £616, and one £11.25 postage charge makes the remaining order balance £627.25. The admission and postage total is therefore £827.25, before travel, food, equipment, insurance, parking or other group costs.

For six ticket holders in one order, the same assumptions give £600 in deposits plus £1,848 in ticket balances plus £11.25 postage: £2,459.25 before other costs. The model restricts a scenario to one order of at most six ticket holders because that is the scope of the cited original-sale per-order figure. It does not automatically calculate multiple orders or assume that a larger party can buy together.

Whether a person actually paid a deposit, whether a balance is due, the payment deadline and what happens after cancellation depend on the particular booking and current organiser terms. The source describes an original-sale balance period in April 2027. Do not apply that historical original-sale window to a later resale unless the organiser confirms it.

Coach-ticket combinations are not modeled as ordinary general admission. The organiser describes coach travel charges and administration separately, and terms can require travel on the booked coach. Obtain an actual official quote and read the specific conditions before comparing it with independent travel. A zero in the other-cost field means no additional amount was entered; it does not mean coach travel or parking is free.

Registration, age and children

The organiser states that everyone aged 13 or over on Wednesday 23 June 2027 who wishes to attend must be individually registered and must have a ticket. It also says children aged 12 and under do not require festival entry tickets or registration and must be accompanied by a ticket-holding adult. Read the latest official family guidance for age evidence, supervision and other requirements.

Admission and transport are different questions. The organiser’s guidance explains that children who do not need a festival ticket can still need a separately booked and paid coach seat when travelling on an official ticket-plus-coach booking. Check the rules for all passengers and any companion before booking. Do not use the general-admission ticket count above as a coach-seat count.

Registration details and ticket photographs belong to individuals. The official information says tickets are non-transferable and checks are carried out at entry. A ticket in someone else’s name or a place in someone else’s order is not automatically transferable to you. Verify the actual identification and registration requirements through the festival’s current official guidance.

Families and groups should decide who is responsible for each child, how to meet if separated and which accessibility or medical support questions need direct confirmation. This guide cannot determine an individual child’s eligibility or replace the organiser’s safeguarding and entry rules.

Two performances and one realistic route

The connection tool takes the end of one hypothetical performance, the start of the next and two user-entered allowances: walking and queue or entry time. If the first ends at 12:00, the next starts at 12:45, walking takes 30 minutes and entry takes 15 minutes, the mathematical margin is zero. If the second starts at 12:44, the model reports a one-minute shortfall.

The calculation is only as useful as its assumptions. Crowds, ground conditions, weather, route closures, stage capacity, inaccessible paths or a performance running late can change the real journey. A stage name or distance is not preloaded, and the tool does not infer walking time from a map. Recheck the route and add a larger allowance if your circumstances require it.

The form is intentionally limited to two same-day times. It rejects a second start earlier than the first end rather than silently deciding that it means the next day. For a late-night performance after midnight, make the calendar-day boundary explicit in your own notes and use a separate calculation. This avoids accidentally treating an overnight plan as a same-day connection.

When an official lineup and actual stage timetable are published, record the source and edition for each performance. Until then, a time typed into the form is a hypothetical scenario. The tool does not claim that a particular artist is performing, that a stage is open, or that you can obtain entry to a capacity-limited area.

Accessibility, travel and what to verify

  1. Check the festival’s current accessibility guidance for the actual edition, ticket type, application process and deadline. Ask about step-free routes, accessible viewing, companion arrangements and transport for your individual needs.
  2. Confirm the specific ticket and transport product, departure location, number of seats and baggage rules with the official issuer. A festival admission ticket and a coach seat are not automatically the same thing.
  3. Check current travel-operator services, arrival instructions, walking routes and return plans. The planner does not know real queue lengths, road restrictions or transport availability.
  4. For international travel, consult the responsible official UK authorities about the passport and entry requirements that apply to your citizenship, residence and purpose. A festival ticket is not a visa.
  5. Recheck official updates before payment and travel. A dated sold-out notice or a planned resale can change, and a programme can be revised.

The organiser says visitors with access requirements should follow its ticket and accessibility application process. The details and supporting documents can be individual and time-sensitive. Contact the festival’s accessibility team through the official information page rather than assuming a general guide can establish an entitlement.
