Release eligibility and online entry are not the same deadline

The Recording Academy lists the product eligibility period as 31 August 2025 through 28 August 2026, and the online entry period as 7 July through 21 August 2026. Its FAQ says entries had to be submitted by 21 August even for recordings released later within the eligibility period.

For example, a hypothetical recording released on 25 August and entered on 20 August falls inside both listed date ranges. The dates alone do not prove that the entry was accepted or that the recording meets distribution, category or other requirements. A release on 25 August entered on 25 August falls inside the release window but outside the listed entry window; the later release deadline does not extend online entry.

Entry, nominee and winner are separate statuses

The Recording Academy describes entries as recordings submitted for consideration. Eligible entries are placed on a first ballot and voting members choose nominees. The final ballot then determines winners under the Academy’s rules. A date-window match, a submission, a nomination and a win are distinct states.

The Academy’s calendar places first-round voting in October 2026, nominations on 16 November and final-round voting from 10 December 2026 to 7 January 2027. Those dates are published milestones, not proof that an individual recording has advanced. Check the official nominees list after the Academy publishes it; this page does not preload predictions as fact.

Two prominent categories are often confused. The Academy says Record of the Year recognises the performance and recording contributions, while Song of the Year recognises the songwriters who wrote and composed the song. A single song can be discussed in both contexts, but the people and work recognised are different. Read the official category rules for exact credit and eligibility questions.

Watching the announcement and the awards

The Academy says the nominations special presentation begins at approximately 04:45 PT / 07:45 ET on 16 November, on Grammy.com and the official Grammys YouTube channel. It lists the complete nomination release and wrap-up at approximately 06:00 PT / 09:00 ET. These are separate points in the announcement, and the Academy says the times can change.

For the ceremony, the Academy lists ABC, Disney+ and Hulu in its US announcement. That does not guarantee a particular international stream, subscription, replay or local channel.
