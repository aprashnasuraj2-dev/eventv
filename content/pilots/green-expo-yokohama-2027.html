The organiser lists the exhibition from 19 March through 26 September 2027. Check current visit slots before committing to travel.

Age reference changes

For March visits the ticket-age reference is 1 April 2026; for later visits it is 1 April 2027. Your age on the visit itself is not the published rule.

Two reservations

The official purchase guide requires a visit date and time reservation and identifies a separate reservation for shuttle or parking facilities.

The ticket information page includes an older note saying date reservations would start later, while the newer FAQ describes reservations. Check the official booking site for the current process and actual available slots.

Find the applicable age band

Enter a date of birth and a proposed visit date. The age is calculated at the organiser’s published reference date, not automatically at the visit. Dates remain in your browser; do not use this as proof of eligibility.

Compare repeated visits

Choose one paid age category, a one-day price and a number of visits. The comparison uses published prices per person and assumes every visit is valid for the product, with no discounts, fees or price changes. The summer pass is valid only 1 July–31 August and cannot replace visits outside that period.

Advance-price sales end 18 March 2027. Regular one-day pricing applies from 19 March. The model does not check that you bought advance tickets, that each visit has an available slot, or that a pass fits your travel dates.

Why the age boundary matters

Suppose a visitor turned 12 on 2 April 2026. For a March 2027 visit, the published reference is 1 April 2026, when the visitor was 11, placing the hypothetical visitor in the child category. For an April 2027 visit the reference is 1 April 2027, when the visitor was 12, placing that same person in the youth category. Their actual birthday at the gate is not the relevant reference under the published rule.

A person born on the reference date reaches their birthday on that date. The calculator validates the calendar date, including leap days, and rejects births after the chosen reference date instead of inventing a rule for newborns. The official FAQ’s free-admission answer is phrased using 1 April 2027; ask the organiser about a March visit with a child born after the earlier reference date or any unclear case.

The organiser says children aged three and under may enter free without an admission ticket. Verify the age reference and any other conditions directly for your own child. The category tool does not issue a ticket, reserve an entry slot or assess eligibility for a disability or group discount.

Read the pass comparison carefully

For an adult, six advance one-day entries cost ¥29,400 at the published per-ticket price, compared with a ¥28,000 season pass. Five such entries cost ¥24,500. The arithmetic break-even is six visits at those assumed prices, but a pass may be less useful if you do not return, need different entry products or cannot obtain your preferred slots.

At the published adult regular one-day price of ¥5,500, the ¥12,000 summer pass reaches price parity or better on the third visit. This comparison is meaningful only when all those visits occur within 1 July–31 August and the product remains valid and available. It ignores special-discount tickets, night tickets, transport, reservation constraints and any changes to price or conditions.

The same category and one-day price are assumed for every modelled visit. If March and later visits place a child in different age bands, calculate those visits separately and verify the pass’s actual category rules with the organiser. “Unlimited entry” describes the organiser’s pass product; it does not promise unlimited available reservations at a preferred time or a specific experience on every visit.

Booking and access are separate decisions

The official purchase guide says to select a ticket, create an account if purchasing through the official site, and reserve the visit date and time. The ticket FAQ also describes a registration and reservation process for paper tickets. Buying an admission product is distinct from securing the intended date and arrival time.

The organiser says shuttle-bus or parking use requires a separate reservation through its access system. Check the exact departure point, timetable, accessible transport, cost, availability and return journey in official guidance. A ticket calculator cannot reserve either transport or admission. Some exhibits may have their own reservation rules; consult the current organiser or exhibitor rather than assuming an entry reservation covers everything.

The FAQ currently describes venue hours of 09:30–21:30 and a planned last admission of 21:00 Japan Standard Time. Hours and product windows can change; verify the official page for your particular visit. A night ticket has a later entry condition and is not equivalent to full-day access.

  1. Choose the actual visit date, relevant age reference and product. Check current availability and payment terms.
