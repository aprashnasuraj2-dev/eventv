Compare daily and full-event ticket assumptions

For one person, enter the number of intended days, your own day-ticket and full-event prices, and a total transport estimate. The same day price is applied to each day and transport is added once to either scenario. This is not a live price or availability check.

Plan the four days

The organiser’s 2027 announcement gives 5–8 April and says the shorter four-day duration is intended to focus the fair. This is an organiser description of the format, not proof that a visitor will secure meetings or achieve procurement savings. Check the current programme, hall plan and opening hours before making appointments.

Spain is the announced partner country. The organiser also describes a Motion & Drives area in Hall 12 and a separate NexGeM event planned alongside the fair. A thematic announcement or adjacent event does not establish that a particular session is included in every admission product. Confirm the venue, ticket scope and actual participant list with the organiser.

For a technical visit, define the problem before requesting a demonstration: required process, throughput, interfaces, environmental conditions, safety constraints, maintenance and integration costs. Distinguish a vendor’s claimed capability from a written specification, independent test and performance in your own operating environment.

Interpret the ticket comparison

If two hypothetical day tickets cost €50 each and a full-event ticket costs €90, the daily option totals €100 before transport while the full-event option totals €90. If estimated transport is €30, the respective scenario totals are €130 and €120. Those numbers are invented, not current fair prices.

The calculator uses one visitor, one equal day-ticket price and the same total transport cost for either option. It does not handle different daily prices, taxes, discounts, multiple visitors or a rail pass automatically. The official ticket page states the admission product is not a public transport ticket, so it is important to check actual transport fares and validity separately.

Do not compare a discounted student or senior product with an unrestricted product unless eligibility and access are equivalent. Check whether a ticket allows re-entry, which event areas or conferences it includes, whether it is transferable and what happens if plans change. A pass covering four dates has no value on dates you cannot attend.

Make the visit useful

  1. Choose a small set of problems and questions; arrange meetings through appropriate official or company channels and keep a practical transfer buffer.
  2. Ask vendors to distinguish a demonstrated product from a roadmap item, and note deployment costs, service commitments and integration limits.
  3. Record whether a contact is a new qualified opportunity or existing pipeline; meetings are not equivalent to sales or profit.
  4. Verify current fair access, transport and accessibility with the organiser and transport operator.
  5. For international travel, consult responsible German and Schengen authorities for your own entry requirements.

The calculator runs locally and does not buy tickets, book meetings or contact vendors. No account, contact or payment information is needed.
