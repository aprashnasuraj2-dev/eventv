Compare two proposed meetings

Enter two same-day appointments in Amsterdam local time, earlier meeting first, plus your own transfer allowance. The tool compares both against the organiser’s listed show hours and reports the gap after transfer. It does not book a meeting or know the hall layout.

An in-hours result is arithmetic against the cited published show hours, not confirmation that a session, booth or entrance is open or available.

Estimate cost per qualified meeting

Enter your own total costs in euros and an assumed number of qualified meetings. Define “qualified” internally before using the ratio. This is a planning scenario, not revenue, profit, return on investment or a guarantee of leads.

The denominator is your assumption. A meeting count is not the same as an opportunity, a sale or collected revenue; add staff time or stand costs if relevant.

Visitor, exhibitor and meeting decisions

IBC identifies RAI Amsterdam as the venue for 10–13 September 2027. A registration-interest form is an expression of interest; it is not a visitor badge, an accepted exhibitor contract or a booked meeting. Check current pass types, prices, opening times, programme and registration status directly with IBC.

The exhibitor page describes options including space-only and shell arrangements. These differ in what is provided and what a company must arrange itself. A space-only footprint can require design, construction, utilities, furniture, connectivity, freight and labour beyond the floor space. A shell package may include some items but still have optional upgrades or exclusions. Ask for a written quote and the applicable 2027 exhibitor manual before making a budget commitment.

A meeting suite or stand enquiry does not guarantee an allocation or a particular location. The organiser’s package page contains illustrative specifications and notes that details are subject to change. Confirm access for your own staff and guests, technical capabilities, permitted branding, accessibility, cancellation and payment terms in your contract.

Use the meeting comparison carefully

Suppose one meeting runs from 11:00 to 11:30 and another from 11:45 to 12:15, with a ten-minute transfer allowance. The model reports a five-minute margin. If the second starts at 11:35, the model reports a five-minute shortfall. It does not know whether the meetings are confirmed, how long the actual walk takes, whether queues block the route or if either appointment runs late.

The tool validates the two meetings against IBC’s published general hours for the selected day. For example, a Monday appointment ending at 16:15 falls outside the listed 16:00 closing time, even if an identical appointment would fit the listed Sunday hours. The comparison does not establish whether a specific hall, conference, entrance or private appointment operates exactly within those general hours.

Appointments must begin and end on the selected day, and the earlier start must be entered first. Two overlapping meetings produce a negative margin. A zero-minute margin is mathematically possible but leaves no room for delay. Add a realistic transfer allowance after checking the current venue map and your mobility needs.

Cost per meeting is not business return

Suppose pass costs are €500, travel and lodging €1,500, and other expenses €500. With ten assumed qualified meetings, the model divides €2,500 by ten and reports €250 per qualified meeting. If only five meetings meet your qualification criteria, the same cost is €500 each. These are hypothetical inputs, not IBC prices or an expected conversion rate.

This ratio can help compare plans only when the cost scope and qualification definition are consistent. It excludes any value of staff time, stand construction, follow-up or opportunity cost unless entered. It does not estimate deal size, probability of closing, gross margin, sales cycle or incremental revenue. A meeting can be valuable without becoming a sale, and a sale can occur for reasons unrelated to the show.

For a more complete internal review, record which costs are fixed or per attendee, whether tax is recoverable, and how you will distinguish new opportunities from existing pipeline. Avoid entering names or contact information into the public page. The form runs in the browser and neither sends the scenario to IBC nor books appointments.

Before travelling

  1. Confirm your registration or exhibitor contract, access dates and the latest official opening hours.
  2. Check the current programme and individual session capacity. A show badge may not include every conference or private event.
  3. Review the venue map, step-free routes and transport-operator notices. Contact IBC or RAI for specific accessibility requirements.
  4. For international travel, consult the responsible Dutch and Schengen authorities for your passport and entry requirements; an event badge is not travel permission.
  5. Leave time for security, hall transfers and follow-up; protect contact details and use your organisation’s own approved systems for customer information.
