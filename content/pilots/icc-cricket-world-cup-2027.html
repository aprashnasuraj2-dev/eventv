Tournament footprint

ICC announced 12 venues across three countries, with the Super Series opening in Windhoek and the final in Johannesburg. Verify updates against the official fixture announcement.

CONFIRMED FORMAT

Super 7 pathway

Two six-team groups feed a Super 7 stage (25 Oct–14 Nov). The top three in each group plus the better fourth-place side advance; points do not carry forward. ICC format explainer.

CONDITIONAL

Pre-seeded teams are not locked

Super 7 slot labels represent possible qualifiers. An alternative qualifying side may take a pre-seeded position. Never book a journey on an assumed knockout participant alone.

UNVERIFIED / DYNAMIC

Tickets, visas and television

Ballot entry does not guarantee tickets. Per-match stock, country-specific entry permission and tournament broadcaster rights are not confirmed by this page. A schedule-launch broadcast is not evidence of 2027 tournament rights.

Counterexample: A match advertised under a pre-seeded B3 team is not necessarily that team's match. If another team qualifies into B3, the scheduled slot remains but its participant changes. Scenario labels below never become official confirmations.

04 / Ticket evidence

What the ballot actually means

The ICC announced a free ticket ballot, not first-come-first-served, with one entry per person per match and requests of up to four tickets per match. The announced closing time was 21 November 2026, 23:59 CAT. A ballot entry is a request, not a confirmed allocation, price or entitlement. Check the official ballot notice and ICC's subsequent ballot update before acting.

05 / Decisions before booking

Questions fans should ask

Are all Super 7 opponents confirmed?

No. Pre-seeded bracket slots depend on actual qualification. This tool marks every user-assigned slot as conditional, even if the name resembles a seeded side.

Does a positive transfer margin mean I can attend both games?

No. It subtracts estimated first-match duration, travel and arrival buffer from kickoff-to-kickoff elapsed time. Match duration, exit delays, border formalities and actual transport availability may make the itinerary impossible.

Can I use the ICC schedule-launch broadcast list to find the 2027 broadcaster?

No. Distribution rights for a launch show are not evidence of rights to the later tournament. Check the ICC and licensed broadcasters closer to the event.

Do all three host countries share a clock?

Their current civil time is UTC+02, but check official time-zone rules again before travel. Your home region may change offset with daylight saving, so use instant-based conversion rather than fixed subtraction.

Does this website sell or reserve tickets?

No. This independent tool stores private notes locally and cannot verify inventory, allocation or eligibility. Follow official ICC channels.
