Compare a group’s own budget

Enter a single assumed ticket price per person, plus group-level fees, parking and other shared costs. This is an estimate, not a quote. Enter zero only when you intend to omit a cost.

The per-person amount assumes an equal split; it does not allocate different ticket types or check availability, taxes, refund conditions or currency conversion.

Work backwards from an assumed arrival

Choose an arrival time at the gate in Indianapolis local time and enter your own travel, queue and additional buffer estimates. This is a planning scenario, not the published gate time or a prediction of traffic.

The tool subtracts all three allowances from your chosen gate arrival and flags a previous calendar day if needed. It does not know where your trip starts, parking walking distance or whether entry is open.

Race day versus the wider Month of May

The organiser identifies May 30 as race day. It also lists qualifying on May 22 and 23. Those are separate dates with separate potential experiences and ticket products; neither the qualifying timetable nor the race-day detailed timetable is established by a simple date label.

The IMS race-day schedule page says the 2027 schedule is to be announced. A visible 12:00 AM appears in the page template alongside that message. Treating that value as the green flag, a gate opening or a broadcast start would turn a placeholder into an invented fact. Revisit the official race-day page when an actual timetable is published, and check later revisions before travelling.

An on-sale announcement does not guarantee a particular seat, lot, price or allocation remains available today. Choose a ticket by what it actually includes

IMS describes reserved seating and other admission products separately in its ticket guidance. A reserved-seat product names a stand and seat; a general admission or viewing-area product may have different access and availability. Check your actual product page for the precise date, permitted areas, age rules, delivery and refund terms. The names of similarly priced products alone do not establish equal access.

The event site also advertises the Snake Pit on race day. Do not infer that any race-day ticket includes concert access or vice versa. Check the current conditions of each product and whether an additional admission purchase is required. The guide does not assign a concert lineup, opening time or age restriction without current supporting terms.

For visitors needing accessible seating, parking, companion arrangements or help reaching a viewing area, contact IMS through its official ticketing and accessibility channels. A map can help orient a group, but a generic map does not confirm that a particular seat or route meets an individual’s requirements or remains available.

Use the numbers as scenarios

Suppose two people each enter a hypothetical $100 ticket price, with $20 in group fees, $40 parking and $30 in other shared costs. The model reports $290 total and $145 per person. These are invented figures, not prices offered by IMS. If one person has a different product, calculate separately or use an appropriate average and record that assumption.

Similarly, if a group wants to reach a gate at an assumed 09:00, enters 60 minutes’ travel, 30 minutes’ queue and a 15-minute buffer, the tool gives 07:15. The calculation is correct only for those assumptions. A parking lot walk, congested road, an earlier gate opening or a service disruption can require a different plan. It cannot determine when a gate opens.

The departure form works in Indianapolis local time and handles going back across midnight. It treats the entered duration as simple elapsed minutes and does not convert between time zones or decide a trip’s starting date. For long-distance or cross-time-zone travel, make a separate itinerary with the actual dates and carrier schedule.

Practical checks before race week

  1. Check the official race-day schedule for published gates, programme and any changes. Save the date of the version you used.
  2. Review the actual ticket product, named seating or permitted area, age conditions, delivery and refund policy before paying.
  3. Check IMS and local authorities for parking, permitted items, traffic restrictions, road closures, accessibility and emergency arrangements. Individual lots and routes can differ.
  4. Plan the last leg from parking or transit to the specific gate, plus a separate exit plan. Queue and walking time can be longer than expected.
  5. For international visitors, confirm current US passport, visa or travel authorisation requirements with the responsible government agency and check carrier terms.

The forms operate in the browser and do not purchase a ticket or contact IMS. Avoid entering payment details, names or other private information. They cannot guarantee access, seats, pricing or a timely arrival.
