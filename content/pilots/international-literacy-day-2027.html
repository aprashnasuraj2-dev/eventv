International Literacy Day 2027

UNESCO observes International Literacy Day annually on 8 September. For 2027, this falls on Wednesday. A worldwide observance does not establish any local school, library or UNESCO event's access, location, registration or opening time.

What is confirmed—and what is not?

Confirmed recurring rule

8 September is the annual observance date. Schools and community organisations may independently choose activities.

Pending for 2027

Theme, UNESCO event programme, local venues, registration, access arrangements and participation are not asserted here.

Historical counterexample: UNESCO's 2025 global celebration in Paris had invitation-only in-person participation, despite its global observance. Do not assume a free public walk-in event in 2027.

    Practical questions

    Is 8 September a public holiday?

    The international observance does not itself make it a public holiday. Check local law and calendars.

    Are literacy events free?

    There is no universal admission policy; check each organiser.

    Does capacity equal teaching quality?

    No. The calculator only sums facilitator-minutes and does not assess educator training, safeguarding, language proficiency, disability access or pedagogy.

    Does UNESCO endorse this tool?

    No. This is an independent offline planning aid.
