    Planning questions, answered

    Is museum entry free on 18 May 2027?

    Not universally. Admission, discounts and reservation requirements are set by each museum; check its current official visitor page.

    Is the 2027 theme confirmed?

    No 2027 theme is asserted in this guide. Historical 2026 material is not a substitute for a new organizer announcement.

    What if a museum celebrates on another date?

    ICOM's observance can include activities around 18 May. Choose the museum's confirmed programme date rather than assuming every museum participates on the same day.

    Can this planner check transit or accessibility live?

    No. It only checks entered time windows and transfer buffers. Confirm travel routes, timed entry and accessibility directly with the venue and local transport provider.

    Can I compare prices in different countries?

    Costs are grouped by entered currency, without foreign-exchange conversion or invented rates. Local taxes, concession rules and group discounts must be verified separately.
