Confirmed versus unverified

Unapproved research draft, not indexable or deployed. This is not Formula 1's official site, a broadcaster, a ticketing agency or a live transport feed.

  1. Formula 1's official 2027 calendar lists Suzuka's Japanese Grand Prix for 9–11 April 2027.
  2. The calendar date alone is not a confirmed session clock time. The converter below deliberately leaves the session start blank and uses the viewer's manually entered time.
  3. Japan Standard Time (JST) is UTC+9 without seasonal clock changes. Some viewer countries observe daylight saving on these April dates.
  4. A time conversion cannot establish a broadcaster's territorial rights, ticket access, a live stream or an actual on-track schedule.

Before using the result

  1. Check a current Formula 1 or promoter session timetable before entering a Suzuka time.
  2. Select the viewer's city and let a date-aware timezone conversion account for DST rather than assuming a static London/New York offset.
  3. Check a legally available broadcaster in the viewer's country and confirm tickets separately if travelling.

Counterexample: Applying London's winter GMT offset to a Japanese Grand Prix in April can produce a result one hour wrong because London normally observes BST.
