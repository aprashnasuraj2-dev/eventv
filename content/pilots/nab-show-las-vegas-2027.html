Where to plan

The organiser lists the Las Vegas Convention Center's West, Central and North Halls for the 2027 exhibition. Individual sessions or outside meetings may differ.

An event-day listing is not proof that the exhibition floor is open. For example, a meeting on 3 April cannot be treated as a visit to the exhibits based on the published 4–7 April exhibit range.

Build a useful show plan

  1. Start with the organiser's event and exhibit dates, then check the current programme and actual session locations.
  2. Group your must-see exhibitors by hall and schedule appointments only after verifying availability and access rights.
  3. Set a realistic transfer allowance between meetings; a convention-centre hall label does not establish the route or walking time.
  4. Check whether your chosen pass covers each conference, exhibit or separately booked event. Verify current prices, fees and cancellation conditions directly.
  5. Keep personal notes and sensitive business information out of shared devices. This form calculates locally and does not register you.

For international travel, check your own entry documents and appointment requirements with the relevant authorities. The show dates alone do not confer permission to travel or attend.

What is established and what remains open

NAB states the show dates as 3–7 April and exhibit dates as 4–7 April 2027. It lists the West, Central and North halls for the exhibition. A specific conference time, speaker, company, show-floor hour, registration fee, ticket entitlement or 2027 attendance figure is not established by these date listings.

News about 2026 attendance can help explain the event's previous scale, but it is not a 2027 result. Confirm the current programme and accessibility arrangements before buying travel.
