Before buying

  1. Identify the exact event, date and venue; a ticket for the convention center is not automatically valid at the arena.
  2. If considering both Jam Session and a same-day Celebrity Game or HBCU Classic, check the included access in the official FAQ and your particular ticket terms to avoid buying the same entry twice.
  3. Compare the checkout total for the number of people, including service fees and costs outside the venue. Check whether children require a separate ticket for the exact event.
  4. Read refund, transfer and entry conditions for the product and seller. The NBA Events FAQ describes non-refundable sales for its covered service; do not automatically apply those terms to every third-party or arena purchase.
  5. Verify times, venue access and transport on the day. Phoenix time zone and your home time zone may follow different daylight-saving rules; use a current time-zone converter for your date.

Start with the event, then the ticket

The NBA’s 2027 ticket FAQ lists several events across different venues during the same Phoenix weekend. It lists the Celebrity Game on Friday and the HBCU Classic on Saturday at Phoenix Convention Center, with Jam Session at the convention center across the weekend. It lists Rising Stars and the Sunday All-Star Game at Mortgage Matchup Center. The same source lists a Music Festival on Friday and Saturday at Margaret T. Hance Park. Dates and venues are event listings, not proof that a particular seat or time is available.

Write down the exact event, day, venue, number of people and seller before comparing offers. Two products described as “All-Star” may cover very different experiences. A convention-center ticket should not be assumed to admit you to an arena event, and a package may have its own product-specific conditions. Open the official listing and the actual issuer’s checkout terms for the chosen product.

For each person, distinguish the ticket’s face amount, service fees, delivery or payment charges, taxes and restrictions. The NBA FAQ says the total price, including fees, is shown before payment for its ticketing flow. Your checkout may have different terms if you purchase another product through a different issuer. The calculator above is a planning tool: it does not connect to those sellers, verify seat locations or buy tickets.

Included access is not automatically reciprocal

The NBA FAQ says a Celebrity Game ticket includes Jam Session access valid that same day. It makes the corresponding same-day statement for an HBCU Classic ticket. A standalone Jam Session ticket does not grant entry to either game. This is a directional relationship: “A includes B” does not imply “B includes A”.

For example, a group considering the Friday Celebrity Game and Friday Jam Session should first check whether its particular Celebrity Game tickets contain the stated Friday Jam Session benefit. If they do, buying a second Friday Jam Session ticket for the same person may duplicate that benefit. The same analysis does not automatically apply to Saturday or Sunday because the stated inclusion is same-day. It also does not establish that every separate ticket or package has identical conditions.

The FAQ also says a Jam Session ticket does not include the Music Festival. A Music Festival product should be checked separately for its own terms; do not reverse the statement and assume every entitlement in the other direction. The calculator returns unknown for package or arena coverage it cannot substantiate. If ticket conditions change after this source check, the issuer’s current terms and actual ticket control.

Included entry does not establish a reserved seat for a different game, a particular entry time, re-entry rights, access for a companion, or any special assistance. Verify those questions with the appropriate organiser and venue. If your group splits between events, each person needs the ticket or credential required for their own activity.

Budget scenarios without invented prices

Our estimate uses the formula people × (ticket quote per person + fees per person) + group travel + group lodging. For example, if two people each enter a hypothetical $100 ticket and $15 in per-person fees, plus $80 group travel and $300 group lodging, the model returns $610. These numbers are only illustrative; they do not describe any 2027 price or offer.

Do not enter the full group ticket price into a field labeled “per person” and then multiply it again. Conversely, if a seller displays one group-level fee rather than a fee per person, add that group-level amount to another group cost or calculate it separately. Be explicit about which amounts are already included in a displayed checkout total so you do not add the same service fee twice.

For a weekend with multiple events, calculate each ticket product separately. Add the results only after checking included benefits and ensuring that group travel and lodging are counted once. The tool models a single ticket type for a group; it does not optimize ticket combinations, account for every child pricing rule or convert international currencies.

Leave time and money for meals, local transport, parking, insurance, currency conversion and taxes that are not already in your quote. A refundable hotel quote and a non-refundable ticket can have different cancellation risks. The organiser’s FAQ describes ticket terms for its covered ticketing flow, including final sales, but those terms should not be generalized automatically to every arena seller or third-party package.

Travel between Phoenix venues

Even when two events are on the same date, check their actual start, end, door and last-entry times. Those details are not supplied by this page. Allow time to leave the first event, reach the right entrance, use transport, clear security and find the second seat. An event that appears to end just before another starts may be impossible to attend in full.

The NBA FAQ identifies Phoenix Convention Center and links to the venue’s transport guidance. Verify current rail, bus, parking and accessibility information with the operator for your actual travel date. An official description of stations or a general service frequency is not a guarantee that your train, route, accessible entrance or parking space will be available when you arrive.

If you need step-free access, accessible seating, hearing assistance or a companion ticket, contact the specific event issuer and venue before purchasing. A pass inclusion statement is not enough to establish that the included venue or seat meets a particular access need. Keep a copy of the response and recheck if the event location or ticket is changed.

Phoenix and your home location may observe clock changes differently. Use an explicit event date and the event’s stated time zone when comparing travel plans, especially if booking from another country. This page does not assert exact 2027 start times or provide a live time conversion. Never infer a broadcast schedule or streaming availability from a venue ticket.

Evidence checklist for each purchase

  1. Event: identify the exact NBA All-Star event, date and venue in the current official listing.
  2. Product: verify the issuing seller, seat or admission category and which same-day benefits are included.
  3. People: confirm the number of tickets and whether a child or companion needs a specific product for that event.
  4. Cost: record the final checkout total and identify any fees already included before using the calculator.
  5. Terms: check transfer, cancellation, refund, re-entry and delivery rules for that exact product rather than assuming a policy for the entire weekend.
  6. Access: confirm transport, entrance, accessibility arrangements and a realistic transfer buffer once detailed schedules are available.
