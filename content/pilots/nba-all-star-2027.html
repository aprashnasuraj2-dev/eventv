Before buying

  1. Identify the exact event, date and venue; a ticket for the convention center is not automatically valid at the arena.
  2. If considering both Jam Session and a same-day Celebrity Game or HBCU Classic, check the included access in the official FAQ and your particular ticket terms to avoid buying the same entry twice.
  3. Compare the checkout total for the number of people, including service fees and costs outside the venue. Check whether children require a separate ticket for the exact event.
  4. Read refund, transfer and entry conditions for the product and seller. The NBA Events FAQ describes non-refundable sales for its covered service; do not automatically apply those terms to every third-party or arena purchase.
  5. Verify times, venue access and transport on the day. Phoenix time zone and your home time zone may follow different daylight-saving rules; use a current time-zone converter for your date.
