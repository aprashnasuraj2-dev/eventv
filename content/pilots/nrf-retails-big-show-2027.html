What is confirmed — and what is not

Unapproved research draft: facts below are specific to the cited edition and publication date. Current ticket inventory, changes, access, live session times, personal eligibility and travel permissions require direct verification.

  1. Confirmed evidence

    The National Retail Federation confirms its New York Retail's Big Show on 10–12 January 2027 at the Javits Center.

  2. Access boundary

    Its official registration page distinguishes retail-member and nonmember categories with date-dependent fees and separate eligibility conditions.

  3. What remains conditional

    An Opening Party is advertised for 9 January; that date is separate from the main 10–12 January conference window.

  4. Planning implication

    Attendees should verify their organisation's category, whether a particular tour or event requires an additional ticket, and the actual rate at checkout.
