Clock labels conflict

One Academy release says 7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT; another calls those clocks EST / PST. The distinction matters on the US daylight-saving transition date.

Compare the two possible clock interpretations

The Academy’s ET/PT wording can be read as local Eastern and Pacific wall time, which under current time-zone data gives 23:00 UTC on 14 March. A literal fixed EST/PST reading gives 00:00 UTC on 15 March. These are interpretations of conflicting labels, not two confirmed broadcast times.

    Understand the two clocks

    The Academy’s May announcement uses 7 p.m. Eastern Time and 4 p.m. Pacific Time for 14 March. Under current IANA time-zone data, daylight saving begins in the United States on that date, so 7 p.m. in New York and 4 p.m. in Los Angeles correspond to 23:00 UTC. A separate Academy announcement uses the abbreviations EST and PST for those same local wall clocks; read literally as fixed standard offsets, they correspond to 00:00 UTC on 15 March.

    The difference is not cosmetic: in London under current rules, those two possible instants fall at 23:00 on 14 March and 00:00 on 15 March. The time-zone tool presents both interpretations rather than picking a winner. Check an updated Academy or broadcaster listing and your country’s official service close to the event. If the Academy intended ET/PT generically in both announcements, the first interpretation could be correct, but the labels alone do not prove that intent.

    A programme start is not automatically the red-carpet start or the time an individual award will be presented. The Academy identifies ABC and Hulu for the US broadcast, but international broadcasters, access requirements, subscription costs and replay windows can differ. Consult the broadcaster in your territory.

    From submissions to nominees and winners

    The Academy’s key-date calendar lists a shortlist announcement on 15 December 2026, nominations announcement on 21 January 2027 and the ceremony on 14 March. It marks dates as subject to change. A film submitted for consideration is not necessarily eligible; an eligible film is not automatically shortlisted; a shortlist is not the final set of nominees; and a nominee is not a winner.

    The Academy also lists separate submission deadlines for different categories and voting windows. A general-entry deadline does not necessarily apply to every category. For an actual submission or voting question, consult the current category rules and Academy calendar; this visitor guide does not decide eligibility or accept entries.

    As of the source check, nominations and winners had not been announced. The personal list above intentionally starts empty. A title entered by a visitor is a viewing note only and cannot be promoted into an Academy claim or a live results feed.
