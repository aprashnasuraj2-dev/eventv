Questions that change your plan

Can a non-member attend on Tuesday or Wednesday?

Member days require a qualifying RHS member to buy the appropriate tickets. The official FAQ allows members to buy up to four member-rate tickets and bring up to three non-member guests; it does not grant walk-in admission to non-members.

Is Friday Chelsea Late included with daytime admission?

Do not assume that. RHS advertises Friday Chelsea Late as a distinct 17:30–22:00 event. Verify your actual ticket product before relying on evening access.

Are the displayed ticket prices guaranteed?

No. The RHS page advertises member tickets from £48 and public tickets from £61 as of 10 November 2026; these are starting rates, not guaranteed inventory, dates or checkout prices.

What if I need step-free access or a companion?

RHS provides accessibility booking information and says one companion may be admitted free for an eligible visitor requiring assistance. Contact the official ticket partner before purchasing; do not assume discounts can be applied retrospectively.

How long should I allow?

RHS visitor advice estimates about 5–7 hours for the main gardens, Great Pavilion and floral displays. This is advice, not a mandatory minimum. Choose a shorter visit if mobility, fatigue or personal interests require it.

Can I rely on the 2026 garden list for 2027?

No. RHS maintains year-specific garden listings. The 2026 exhibitors and garden awards are historical, not confirmed 2027 participation.
