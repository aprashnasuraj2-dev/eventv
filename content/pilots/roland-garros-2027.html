What is confirmed — and what is not

Unapproved research draft: facts below are specific to the cited edition and publication date. Current ticket inventory, changes, access, live session times, personal eligibility and travel permissions require direct verification.

  1. Confirmed evidence

    The FFT's 2027 ticket conditions state that the tournament runs from 17 May to 6 June 2027; the final phase starts on 23 May.

  2. Access boundary

    The official conditions distinguish day and night tickets for Philippe-Chatrier court for specified dates. They are not blanket interchangeable entries.

  3. What remains conditional

    An organiser notice dated 18 September 2026 warned that individual 2027 tickets had not yet opened at that point and directed readers to official channels. That dated notice must not be mistaken for a live sales status.

  4. Planning implication

    Court admission, stadium entry and hospitality rights depend on the actual product and date, not on the ticket's broad event label.

Exact session scope matters: The FFT's 2027 terms specify a Philippe-Chatrier Day Session / Night Session split for 23 May–2 June 2027. Opening Week (17–22 May) and the last four days (3–6 June) must not automatically be treated as separately ticketed night sessions. Court rights and arrival times depend on the exact credential.
