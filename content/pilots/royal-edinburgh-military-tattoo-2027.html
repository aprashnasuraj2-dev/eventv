Royal Edinburgh Military Tattoo 2027 · 6–28 August · Castle Esplanade. Official dates confirmed; seat availability is not.

Official organiser tickets and accessibility ↗

What is confirmed

Official 2027 dates: 6–28 August. Monday–Friday performances ordinarily begin at 21:30; Saturdays have 18:15 and 21:30 performances; Friday 27 August also has an 18:15 performance. Sundays have none. Check your booked ticket for changes.

Shows are approximately 90 minutes; this is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed end time.

Important distinction

Castle daytime admission and Tattoo evening admission are separate. Accessible seating must be arranged with the official box office. Tickets, weather, queue time, and transport connections are not supplied live here.

Does a castle ticket include the Tattoo?

No. These are distinct admission products. Verify each on the official seller's site.

Is a Sunday show available?

Not under the currently published 2027 schedule. The planner refuses Sunday selections.

Does the planner reserve seats?

No. It works entirely in your browser and cannot check inventory or issue tickets.
