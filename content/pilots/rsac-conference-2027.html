Source boundaries and counterexamples

CONFIRMED: Organizer and Moscone Center both list 5–8 April 2027, San Francisco. PENDING: Exact session admission, pass entitlements, meeting rooms and current registration checkout totals. HISTORICAL: 2026 attendance or session numbers are not 2027 forecasts.

Counterexample: An 11:00 meeting end and 11:15 next start look nonoverlapping, but 20 minutes walking plus 15 minutes security produces a 20-minute deficit. A valid expo pass does not establish entry to a separately restricted conference program.

San Francisco uses America/Los_Angeles time; the April 2027 dates fall in Pacific Daylight Time. This utility accepts local clock times and does not convert invitation time zones.
