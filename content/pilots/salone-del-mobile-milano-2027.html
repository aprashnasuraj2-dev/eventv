Six days, different access

Trade visitors

13–18 April. Select the correct category and verify the organiser's professional conditions.

Students and furnishing suppliers

16–18 April. Supplier access refers to furnishing-sector suppliers and manufacturers, not all suppliers.

General public

17–18 April. Visitor opening hours are listed as 09:30–18:30, Milan local time.

The fair is at Fiera Milano Rho. A city design-week event can be at a different venue and may have separate dates and admission. Allow your own transfer time and confirm its organiser's details.

Build a realistic stand plan

Enter your own assumptions for one same-day visit. The tool compares your day and category with the organiser's general access summary, then calculates whether the number of stands fits your chosen window. It does not validate a ticket, professional status, age, queues or actual walking times.

Capacity = visit minutes − (stands × minutes per stand + total breaks and transfers). A positive balance is a planning margin, not a promise of availability.

Children, tickets and exceptions

The organiser describes a general under-10 restriction for this commercial fair, but gives a specific exception on public opening days: children aged 0–9 can enter free when a parent signs a release at reception. On those public days, its visitor page says ages 10–13 need a reduced ticket from the fair ticket offices; it describes visitors aged 14 and over under the student category with access on 16–18 April. Check the full current terms for your family and tickets before travelling.

The admissions page explicitly says 2027 rates are not yet available and labels the figures shown there as 2026 prices. A prior-year price is not a 2027 quote. The access calendar does not prove personal eligibility, available tickets, session entry or a right to purchase.

Plan more than the headline dates

  1. Pick your access category and verify the correct documentation, ticket date and age requirements.
  2. Identify the exhibitors or areas you actually want to see. Their schedules, appointments and available 2027 floor information need separate checks.
  3. Estimate arrival, bag or accessibility needs, breaks and transfers before assigning time to each stand. Include a contingency for queues or a meeting running late.
  4. Keep an off-site city event separate from the fairground visit; confirm its location, booking and end time with its own organiser.
  5. Before paying, check the current total, taxes, refund terms and the exact product on the official booking page.

Counterexample: a general visitor can fit an all-day plan within Friday's hours but is still outside the published public-access days. The capacity and access checks answer different questions.
