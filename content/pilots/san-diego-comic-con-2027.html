Start with the badge rules

Event dates

Comic-Con lists 22–25 July 2027 at the San Diego Convention Center, with Preview Night on Wednesday 21 July.

Open registration

The organiser schedules the public badge sale for 14 November 2026. A Member ID and waiting-room entry do not guarantee a badge.

Preview dependency

Preview Night is not standalone: all four daily badges are required, and Preview Night still depends on availability.

The sale and prices below are dated organiser information, not a live inventory feed. Verify the latest rules, eligibility and sale status directly before purchase.

Open registration, step by step

The organiser lists open registration on Saturday 14 November 2026, with the waiting room opening at 07:30 PST and the sale beginning at 09:00 PST. Its guide says people who enter before the sale begins are placed in a randomized queue. Entering later in that window does not reduce priority relative to someone who arrived earlier. People entering for the first time after 09:00 are placed at the back of the line.

A waiting-room position is not a purchase and selection for a registration session is not a guarantee that every day remains available. The organiser says daily badges can sell out while in a cart. An order acknowledgement, later payment processing and badge confirmation are separate steps; read the current instructions and payment conditions on the official portal.

The organiser says a valid, confirmed Member ID is required for buyers and for each eligible member for whom an adult or junior badge is purchased. Its open-registration instructions describe how to purchase for other eligible members using their exact Member ID and last name, with a limit depending on whether the buyer also purchases for themselves. Do not enter account names, IDs or payment details into this independent planner.

Returning registration took place earlier. Eligibility for that sale and open registration are different; a confirmed Member ID alone does not imply eligibility for every registration category. Consult the organiser’s current terms, account instructions and official communications rather than relying on a third-party badge offer.

Prices and Preview Night

The organiser lists adult Thursday, Friday and Saturday badges at $88 each, Sunday at $67 and Preview Night at $67. For the listed reduced category it shows $44 for each of Thursday through Saturday, $34 for Sunday and $34 for Preview Night. All purchases are subject to a $15 handling fee per member. Check the latest official page before ordering.

For an adult hypothetical purchase of all four days, the daily amounts add to $331. One $15 per-member fee yields $346. Adding Preview Night at $67 gives $413, assuming all five selections are available and purchased for the same member. A separate attendee has their own eligible rate and per-member fee; the tool estimates one member at a time.

The organiser says Preview Night is not a standalone product. A person must have or purchase all four daily badges and Preview Night must still be available. An existing four-day holder has a specific process in the registration guide; the calculator’s checkbox rule models only a new all-days scenario, not that separate purchase flow.

The reduced column covers eligible juniors, active-duty U.S. military and seniors under the published terms. The organiser specifies that the military rate does not extend to veterans and dependents. Individual eligibility, identification, taxes and payment outcomes are outside the tool’s scope.

Children and the 2026 age reference

The child badge policy uses age on 31 July 2026 to determine the transition for Comic-Con 2027. A child who was 12 or younger on that reference date may attend free with a paying adult under the policy, even if their age is different by the 2027 event. Someone who was already 13 by that date needs a Member ID and a purchased badge under the cited rule.

The organiser says up to two children may be registered per paid adult, on site, and that the child must be present. It says children should not create a Member ID for a complimentary child badge and that the adult provides emergency contact information during registration. Check the current official policy and badge confirmation for your own family before travel.

The child-to-junior validation window described for returning registration has closed, according to the organiser. That statement concerns returning-registration validation and does not itself decide whether a particular junior can participate in open registration. Ask the organiser about any account or eligibility issue.

Plan after obtaining a badge

  1. Verify the actual days and badge confirmation, payment status and refund terms. A reservation attempt is not a completed purchase.
  2. Check the official 2027 programme when published. Do not assume panels, guests, room locations or line policies from a previous edition carry forward.
  3. Review the organiser’s current accessibility, transport, shuttle, parking, permitted-items and hotel guidance for the dates you will attend.
  4. For international travel, verify current US entry requirements with the responsible authorities and confirm your carrier and accommodation terms.
  5. Leave time between user-selected activities for movement and queues; a badge or a programme listing does not guarantee admission to a capacity-limited panel.

The tools work in the browser and do not contact Comic-Con or buy badges. Avoid entering names, Member IDs, contact details or payment information.
