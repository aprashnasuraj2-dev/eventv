Prepare a real decision

  1. Verify the specific badge days and Member ID requirements before making non-refundable bookings.
  2. Separate hotel reservations, badge purchases and paid offsite experiences.
  3. Treat any programme, celebrity appearance or panel capacity as contingent until the organiser publishes and confirms it.
