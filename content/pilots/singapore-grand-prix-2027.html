Before using the result

  1. Enter the actual published session end or a clearly labeled hypothetical end time, and avoid assuming previous-year finishing times.
  2. Verify the exact gate and transit route plus service advisories, then enter their confirmed or estimated intervals.
  3. Test a delayed-exit case with a larger security/queue buffer; a small positive spare time may still be operationally fragile.

Counterexample: A ticket valid for Sunday racing cannot guarantee that a user-entered last metro departure exists after a delayed end or a 45-minute exit queue.
