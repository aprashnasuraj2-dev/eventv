Festival and parade are different plans

Five festival days

The organiser lists city-centre events from 13 to 17 March; the detailed programme and individual booking conditions can change.

Parade begins at noon

The organiser states that the national parade starts at 12:00 on Wednesday 17 March. That is not the time it passes every viewing point.

Standing or grandstand

The organiser says no ticket is required for street viewing; seated grandstand products are separate, limited and subject to availability.

The parade page describes the start as Parnell Square while the visitor FAQ names Granby Row. Check the latest official map for the precise route and your viewing location.

Where and when the parade happens

The organiser’s parade page describes a route from the north city centre down O’Connell Street, across O’Connell Bridge and through the south side to the Cuffe Street/Kevin Street junction. It says the parade begins at noon on 17 March. The official FAQ describes the northern start as Granby Row, while the parade page uses Parnell Square. The precise operational starting point should be confirmed on the latest official map and event-day notices.

The start time is a single event-wide anchor. A moving parade takes time to reach a place farther along the route and may experience interruptions; this guide does not turn noon into a promised pass-by time for every street. The organiser’s FAQ says the parade takes approximately two hours to pass, which is a general description rather than a location-specific timetable.

For example, if you choose a 90-minute lead before noon, a 40-minute journey and 20 minutes of extra buffer, the calculator returns a hypothetical 10:30 arrival and 09:30 departure. Those are your selected assumptions, not official travel advice. If your actual trip takes longer or a crossing is closed, you may need a different plan.

Free viewing, seats and other festival events

The organiser states that no ticket is required to stand along the parade route. It separately sells limited grandstand seating. An available product may have a defined location, accessibility conditions, age policy, delivery method or hospitality component. Check the exact product and total cost with the official box office rather than assuming all seats are the same.

The grandstand page said 2027 tickets were available when checked, but this guide does not monitor current inventory or provide a live price. A hypothetical zero ticket cost in the calculator represents a free-standing scenario; it does not assert that transport, food, accommodation or a reserved place is free.

The five-day festival includes events in addition to the 17 March parade. The organiser’s visitor FAQ says many are free, some free events require registration, and some have a charge. Verify each event’s date, location, access and booking requirements on its current listing rather than assuming a parade ticket covers a separate performance.

Accessibility and a realistic route

The organiser describes wheelchair-accessible parade viewing areas and a relaxed parade space, with applications planned to open in mid-January 2027. Availability, capacity, locations and eligibility should be confirmed through the current organiser guidance. The festival also describes accessible toilets and an Accessoloo; verify the location and operating arrangements before relying on them.

For a group with mobility, sensory or medical needs, ask the organiser about the specific viewing area, companion arrangements, routes around closures and facilities. A route shown on a map is not necessarily step-free on event day. The festival notes that indoor programme venues can have different accessibility arrangements.

The organiser expects road closures and says detailed 2027 closure information will be provided closer to the festival. Check the current city and transport-operator notices, including pedestrian crossings, stop changes and your return journey. Avoid treating an unverified map route or a previous year’s timetable as a guarantee.

  1. Check the current event listing and parade map, including any change to the starting area.
  2. Decide whether you are planning public standing, a grandstand product or an accessible area, and verify the applicable booking process.
  3. Allow for transport, walking and crowds, and agree on a meeting point with your group.
  4. For international travel, verify current Irish entry rules with the responsible authorities and confirm carrier terms.
  5. Check weather and organiser notices before leaving; a calculated departure cannot predict queues or closures.
