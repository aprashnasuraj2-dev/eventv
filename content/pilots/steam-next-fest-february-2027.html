The short answer

Steam Next Fest February 2027 runs for one week

Valve’s Steamworks documentation confirms the February 2027 edition begins on Monday, February 22 at 10:00 AM PST (18:00 UTC) and ends on Monday, March 1 at 10:00 AM PST (18:00 UTC). Steam describes Next Fest as a multi-day showcase for upcoming unreleased games with playable demos. Players can try demos, interact with developers and watch livestreams.

Player guide

How Steam Next Fest works

01

Browse upcoming games

Next Fest is for unreleased games with public playable demos. Steam organizes participating games using registration categories and store tags.

02

Try demos for free

The core activity is trying playable demos before the full games launch. A demo may stay available after the event, but that decision belongs to each developer.

03

Wishlist the standouts

Wishlisting is the simplest way to keep track of a game after a demo session and get notified through Steam when the title later releases.

04

Revisit discovery midweek

Valve says most Next Fest sections begin broadly randomized, while later recommendations become more personalized from player behavior during the event.

A useful discovery tactic

Don’t judge the whole festival from your first screen.

Because Steam’s presentation can change as recommendation signals accumulate, sample broadly early in the week and return later. Your second pass may surface a different set of demos than day one.

For developers

Can a game join Steam Next Fest more than once?

No. Valve’s current Next Fest rules say a title may participate in only one Steam Next Fest. For the February 2027 edition, the game must be unreleased when the Fest concludes, have a public store page and a publicly playable demo by the event start, among other eligibility requirements.

Show the main eligibility points
  • Steamworks developer account in good standing.
  • Published public base-game store page.
  • Publicly playable demo by the time the festival begins.
  • Full game releases only after the February Fest concludes.
  • The title has not already participated in another Next Fest.

Developers should always use Steamworks as the authoritative eligibility source because Valve can change requirements.

FAQ

Steam Next Fest February 2027 questions

When is Steam Next Fest February 2027?

It starts February 22, 2027 at 10:00 AM PST (18:00 UTC) and ends March 1 at the same time.

Are Steam Next Fest demos free?

Steam Next Fest centers on publicly playable demos of upcoming games. Valve describes the event as a way for players to try demos before games launch; individual full games can still be paid products when released.

Does every demo disappear when Next Fest ends?

No universal rule says every demo must disappear. Valve specifically advises developers to tell players if they plan to deactivate a demo after the Fest. Availability therefore depends on the developer.

Is the February 2027 demo lineup available yet?

This page does not currently treat a final lineup as published. We will add specific games only after an applicable Valve/Steam event source is available and verified.

How does Steam decide which demos I see?

Valve says Next Fest uses the categories selected by developers plus Steam Tags. The first few days are broadly randomized in most areas, with personalized recommendations becoming more prominent after Steam gathers event behavior.

When is the next Steam Next Fest after February?

Valve currently lists the next 2027 edition for June 14–21, 2027.
