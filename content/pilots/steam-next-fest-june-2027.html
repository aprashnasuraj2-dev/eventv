The short answer

When is Steam Next Fest June 2027?

Valve’s Steamworks documentation confirms that Steam Next Fest: June 2027 begins Monday, June 14 at 10:00 AM PDT (17:00 UTC) and concludes Monday, June 21 at 10:00 AM PDT. Steam describes Next Fest as a weeklong event for unreleased games with playable demos, developer interaction and livestreams.

✓
What is already safe to plan around?

The event timing, developer registration deadline, press-preview date and core eligibility rules are official. The final player-facing June 2027 lineup is not treated as confirmed here until Valve publishes the relevant event catalog.

Discovery strategy

Why a seven-day plan beats opening 30 demos on day one

DAY 1–2

Sample broadly

Valve says most locations in Next Fest are broadly randomized during the first few days, except explicitly named lists and personalized segments.

DAY 3–5

Return for personalized discovery

After a few days, logged-in users can see carousels personalized from behavior during Next Fest. Revisiting can surface different demos than your first session.

DAY 6–7

Finish high-priority demos

Developers may choose to deactivate demos after the Fest. Valve specifically tells developers to communicate that plan to players, so prioritize anything with an end-of-Fest warning.

AFTERWARD

Use the wrap-up as a second lens

Valve says the June 21 wrap-up will feature games with the most-played demos. That is popularity evidence, not a quality ranking.

For developers

Why June can be a one-shot release-timing decision

Valve says a game may participate in only one Steam Next Fest. For June 2027, an eligible title must have a public base-game store page, a publicly playable demo by the time the Fest starts, remain unreleased until after June 21 and meet Steamworks account requirements. There is no requirement that the full game launch immediately after the Fest.

Show the June 2027 eligibility summary
  • Steamworks developer account in good standing.
  • Public base-game store page.
  • Publicly playable demo by the event start.
  • Full game release after the Fest concludes on June 21.
  • No prior participation in another Next Fest.

Developers should use the Steamworks edition page as the authoritative source because Valve may revise implementation details.

FAQ

Steam Next Fest June 2027 questions

When is Steam Next Fest June 2027?

It starts June 14, 2027 at 10:00 AM PDT (17:00 UTC) and ends June 21 at the same time.

Is June Next Fest different from February Next Fest?

The core format is the same: upcoming unreleased games with playable demos. The dates, lineup, developer deadlines and local time-zone conversion differ. June starts at 10 AM PDT, while February starts at 10 AM PST.

Are all Steam Next Fest demos free?

Next Fest centers on publicly playable demos for upcoming games. The full games may be paid products when they later release.

Do demos disappear on June 21?

Not necessarily. Valve tells developers to tell players if they plan to deactivate a demo after the Fest, which means availability can differ by game.

How does Steam decide which demos I see?

Valve says registration categories and Steam Tags help organize the Fest. Most areas are broadly randomized in the first few days, while logged-in recommendations become more personalized after Steam gathers event behavior.

When does the press preview start?

Valve currently lists June 3, 2027 at 10:00 AM PDT.

When is the Next Fest after June 2027?

Valve says the next edition is planned for October 2027, but this page does not assert exact October dates because the edition-specific dates have not been confirmed in the sources reviewed here.
