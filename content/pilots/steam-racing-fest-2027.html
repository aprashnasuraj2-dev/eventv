Buyer rule #1

“Controller supported” is not proof your racing wheel works properly.

Steam Input is designed to help controller-supported games work across many gamepads. Valve’s developer guidance specifically says that if a game supports a flight stick or racing wheel, developers should leave Generic/DirectInput controllers unchecked for Steam Input because Steam does not support remapping those devices. That makes native wheel support, device model, force feedback, pedals and shifter behavior separate evidence questions.

Gamepad support ≠ wheel supportXbox/PlayStation controller compatibility cannot certify a Logitech, Thrustmaster, Fanatec or other wheel.
Wheel detected ≠ force feedback worksInput mapping, FFB, pedal axes, clutch, H-pattern/sequential shifter and LEDs can have different support levels.
Base game support ≠ demo supportValve says demo pages should list only the features/modes actually present in the demo.
Discounted ≠ best-value editionBase game, Deluxe/Premium editions and DLC bundles need like-for-like total-cost comparison.

Counterexample: a game can advertise full gamepad support and still require separate native DirectInput handling for a racing wheel. “Works with controller” is therefore not enough evidence for a sim-rig purchase.

What “eligible for Racing Fest” actually means

Valve’s generic themed-sale documentation says eligibility is based on the game’s store tags plus the gameplay represented on the store page. For Racing, Valve specifically uses the Racing tag as part of consideration and requires the game to be primarily about the theme. A non-racing game with only a racing mini-game is the explicit counterexample and is not eligible on that basis.

Discount behavior / buyer implications

Racing Fest is not a seasonal-sale cooldown exception.

Valve’s current Discounting rules require 30 days between standard discounts. The only cooldown exceptions are the four major seasonal sales: Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. A themed event such as Racing Fest follows the normal cooldown. Standard discounts are currently constrained to 10%–95% and 1–14 days.

Discount floor / ceiling10%–95%

Current standard discount limits; title-specific Racing Fest discounts remain publisher-selected.

Standard duration1–14 days

Official themed-sale discounts can cover the event, but exact publisher choices vary.

Discount cooldown30 days

Seasonal-sale exceptions do not make Racing Fest itself an exception.

Wishlist trigger20%+ baseline

Can trigger notifications if package/duration/account conditions are also met.

Do not infer deal depth from participation. A Racing Fest participant can be full price, free-to-play, unreleased, demo-enabled or discounted; Valve does not set one universal discount percentage.

Steam discovery mechanics

The sale page is curated into sections, but placement is not guaranteed.

Valve says themed sales normally contain top-level areas such as Discounted, VR, Free to Play, Demos and Upcoming, plus theme-specific subsections. Games inside sections can be ordered using heuristics such as player interest and popularity, while other sections can be more random. Valve does not guarantee specific featuring or positions.

Steam Racing Fest 2027 questions

When is Steam Racing Fest 2027?

Valve’s Upcoming Steam Events page lists April 12 through April 19, 2027.

What time does Steam Racing Fest 2027 start?

The exact official clock is not verified here. Valve’s dedicated Racing Fest 2027 documentation route currently errors, so this page does not treat common community “10am PT” assumptions as an official event time.

Does every game have to be discounted?

No. Valve says discounts are optional for themed sales, though discounted released games receive the most prominent placement.

Will there be demos?

Generic themed-sale rules allow eligible demos for released or unreleased games unless the event-specific documentation says otherwise. No final Racing Fest demo lineup is asserted here.

Does Steam controller support mean my steering wheel works?

No. Valve specifically notes that racing wheels are not remapped by Steam Input in the same way generic gamepads are. Verify your exact wheel, force feedback, pedals and shifter per game.

Can an adventure game with one racing mission join?

Not on that basis. Valve explicitly says the game must primarily be about the event theme; a racing mini-game inside another genre is its example of an ineligible case.

Do Racing Fest discounts trigger wishlist emails?

A 20%+ discount can trigger wishlist notifications if it affects the lowest-priced package, runs more than eight hours and other user/app communication conditions are satisfied. Delivery is not guaranteed.
