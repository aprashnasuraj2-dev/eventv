Buying guide / 01

Start with a decision, not a discount percentage

Steam's Spring Sale is a Steam-wide seasonal promotion, unlike a genre-specific Fest. Participating game discounts are submitted by publishers, subject to Valve's eligibility and discount rules. A listed event does not mean every title will be on sale, nor that every discount is its historical low.

Own it already?

Check your library and DLC ownership before comparing an edition or bundle.

Will you play it soon?

Include available play hours and your existing backlog. A low price alone is not a reason to buy.

Is the comparison fair?

Match region, currency, store edition, tax treatment and bundle contents.

Never interpret the examples or initial field values in these tools as live prices. Every number comes from you.

    FAQ / verified vs unknown

    Questions people actually need answered

    When is Steam Spring Sale 2027?

    Valve lists March 18 to March 25, 2027. Exact start/end times are not confirmed in the official event calendar checked on November 5, 2026. Do not convert an assumed clock into your timezone.

    Will my wishlisted game definitely be discounted?

    No. Publisher participation and discount eligibility vary by product. The official calendar establishes the event dates, not discounts on individual titles.

    Does 50% off prove it is the best historical price?

    No. It describes a reduction relative to a listed reference price. Verify dated same-region history and total checkout cost. Price histories are not provided by EventV.

    Can I refund a discounted game?

    Steam generally offers refunds for games/software within two weeks and under two hours played, subject to terms and exceptions. You can request consideration even outside these thresholds. See Steam's official refund policy.

    Do I need to buy before March 25?

    If you want an advertised sale discount, verify the offer remains active in your Steam store. We do not know the exact end clock, and Steam says expired-sale discounts cannot simply be applied retroactively.
