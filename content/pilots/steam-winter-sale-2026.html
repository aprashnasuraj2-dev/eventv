Buying discipline

A sale percentage is not the same as value

Steam shows the current transaction price, but whether a game is worth buying depends on your own budget, backlog, hardware and intended play time. Treat the percentage as one input—not the decision.

01

Start from your wishlist

Steam can notify you when wishlisted games go on sale. Valve’s developer documentation says discounts of 20% or more can automatically trigger wishlist notifications, subject to the player’s notification settings.

02

Check the final price

A large percentage on a high regular price can still exceed your budget. Calculate the actual cost first, especially when comparing editions or bundles.

03

Separate “want” from “maybe”

Create a small must-play queue and a larger watchlist. This keeps impulse purchases from consuming the budget before higher-priority games appear.

04

Revisit the store

Valve says the seasonal-sale front page refreshes regularly so different featured games have visibility throughout the event. The first day is not the only time to browse.

How Steam seasonal sales work

Why the Winter Sale is different from a small publisher discount

Steam-wide seasonal event

Valve runs four major seasonal sales each year. The store front page is redesigned around the sale and discounted titles can appear in specials sections.

Regularly refreshed discovery

Valve says the front page refreshes options throughout the sale so featured games can receive visibility across the event rather than only a single day.

Special discount-rule exception

Seasonal-sale discounts are exempt from Steam’s normal 30-day discount cooldown. Release and price-increase cooldowns still apply.

Wishlist notification relevance

Valve says a discount of at least 20% can automatically trigger wishlist notifications. That does not guarantee every player sees an email or push notification.

2026 Steam Awards

The Winter Sale also includes voting

Valve’s Winter Sale page says the event is for shopping for games and voting for favorites in the 2026 Steam Awards. This page will add voting deadlines, finalist categories and official voting links only after the applicable player-facing details are verified.

Developer note

Can every game join the Steam Winter Sale?

Steam’s upcoming-events documentation says games released at least 30 days before the seasonal sale start can participate with a discount. Valve’s discount rules also say a product cannot be discounted within 30 days of a release or price increase; seasonal sales are exempt from the ordinary discount cooldown, but not those release or price-increase restrictions.

Show the relevant discount-rule summary
  • Normal discounts cannot be less than 10% or greater than 95%.
  • Seasonal sales are exceptions to the normal 30-day discount cooldown.
  • A recent release or base-price increase can still block eligibility.
  • Developers should use Steamworks Discount Management as the authoritative setup interface.

FAQ

Steam Winter Sale 2026 questions

When does Steam Winter Sale 2026 start?

December 17, 2026 at 10:00 AM Pacific Standard Time, which is 18:00 UTC.

When does Steam Winter Sale 2026 end?

January 4, 2027 at 10:00 AM Pacific Standard Time (18:00 UTC).

How long is Steam Winter Sale 2026?

It runs from December 17 through January 4, spanning the Christmas and New Year holiday period.

Will every game on Steam be discounted?

No. Steam’s seasonal sale is broad, but participation and discount size are set by eligible publishers/developers. Do not assume a specific game will be discounted until its live store price changes.

Will Steam notify me when a wishlisted game is discounted?

Steam supports wishlist sale notifications. Valve’s developer documentation says discounts of 20% or more can automatically trigger wishlist notifications, but delivery also depends on the player’s settings and Steam’s notification systems.

Is a 50% discount always better than 25%?

Not necessarily for your budget. Compare the final price, edition contents and whether you intend to play it. The calculator above shows the arithmetic but does not measure historical value.

When is the next Steam seasonal sale?

Valve currently lists Steam Spring Sale 2027 for March 18–25, followed by Summer Sale 2027 from June 24–July 8.
