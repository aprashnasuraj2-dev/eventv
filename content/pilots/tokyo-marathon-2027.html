Official evidence and remaining uncertainties

Edition-specific evidence

Not index-approved or published. No live prices, session availability, ticket purchases, travel directions, eligibility verification or affiliation with the organiser.

  1. The Tokyo Marathon Foundation confirms Sunday 7 March 2027 for the 20th edition and identifies a 40,000-runner field.
  2. Official entry guidance requires in-person packet pickup before race day with photo ID; it does not offer race-day runner registration.
  3. General runners, wheelchair athletes, charity participants and other classes have separate conditions; a numeric pace alone does not establish eligibility.
  4. A dated lottery result or past application window is not an open-entry invitation; the official participant account is authoritative.

Before relying on a result

  1. Check accepted entry status and personal pickup dates independently of this pacing model.
  2. Read the applicable 2027 runner guide and weather/course advisory; do not treat a constant pace as a physiological prediction.
  3. Distinguish EXPO events from the race, and allow time for identity checks and staging.

Counterexample: A pace consistent with a general participant limit cannot qualify a runner for a wheelchair division or obtain an entry bib.
