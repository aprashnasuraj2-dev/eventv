Total solar eclipse: plan the experience, not a guess

NASA confirms a total eclipse across a narrow path in southern Spain, North Africa and the Arabian Peninsula. A partial eclipse across a wider region is not totality. Local contact times and travel access must be independently verified.

Practical questions

Does a country on the path guarantee totality?

No. Totality occupies a narrow corridor. The rest of that country may see only partial eclipse or none.

Can I use the global maximum as my local contact time?

No. Global greatest eclipse is not your site's start, maximum or end. Consult a site-specific official prediction.

Does this page sell viewing access?

No. It stores user-entered planning notes only. No prices, access rights or weather forecast are implied.
