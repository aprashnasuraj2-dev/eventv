    Decision checklist

    One viewing point or multiple?

    A single location with generous arrival time may be more practical than moving between start and finish on the same day.

    Public transport assumptions

    Normal timetables may not apply during road closures. Confirm return transport and step-free access with local operators.

    Opening ceremony ≠ roadside access

    Separately ticketed ceremonies and roadside viewing have different entry conditions; do not infer one from the other.
