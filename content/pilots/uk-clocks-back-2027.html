01 / Evidence first

What is established — and what isn’t

Every confirmed claim below has a relevant authority in the reference list. Dates can be established without proving a future business opening schedule, travel service or personal eligibility. Your own evidence, particularly around 2027 rules, should be rechecked before any financial or logistical commitment.

GOV.UK

The UK changes from BST to GMT on the last Sunday in October, at 02:00 BST → 01:00 GMT.

Repeated time

01:30 occurs twice, once under BST and once under GMT, separated by one real hour.

Ireland

Irish winter begins at 01:00 GMT on the last Sunday in October, aligned to the same UTC transition.

Cross-Atlantic

Most participating U.S. zones do not switch until November 7, 2027.

Counterexample that changes the decision:

London 01:30 occurs twice on October 31, while New York 01:30 that same date occurs once under its still-active daylight time.

Confirmed / rule-derived

The calendar statements and published statutory time rules above are supported by the identified primary authorities as of the editorial check date. All calculations label their assumptions.

Pending / individual

2027 branch opening times, transit services, travel disruptions, seats, merchandise offers, accommodation and individual eligibility remain unknown unless separately verified from the controlling provider.

04 / Detailed decision playbook

Five practical failure modes — and how to avoid them

1

Two 01:30 entries, not a duplicated bug

The UK’s clocks go back on October 31, 2027. At 02:00 BST, local time returns to 01:00 GMT; therefore '01:30' exists at two separate UTC instants.

A user selecting 01:30 without a zone offset gives an incomplete instruction to scheduling software. The tool reports two alternatives and makes their UTC identity visible.

Boundary / counterexample: A reliable calendar, ledger or operational alert stores the resolved instant. 'First 01:30' and 'second 01:30' are not interchangeable.
2

The Halloween coincidence

October 31 is Halloween, and the UK clock transition occurs earlier that morning. This does not establish trick-or-treat hours or local event arrangements later on Sunday.

Parents and organizers planning late-night gatherings spanning 01:00 need a clear finish time. Community event times remain organizer-local, not national.

Boundary / counterexample: One event may start at 00:30 BST and finish at 01:30 GMT, meaning two physical hours despite appearing to last one.
3

A one-week difference from US clocks

The UK switches October 31, 2027; observing U.S. states return to standard time November 7. Cross-Atlantic recurring calls can therefore shift by one hour temporarily.

Do not assume a static London–New York difference. Convert each meeting’s date using actual named zones; treat Phoenix, Hawaii and most of British Columbia as special non-transition cases.

Boundary / counterexample: A counterexample is a fixed-UTC video call: its London display changes on October 31 even if New York’s local display does not.
4

Timekeeping beyond meetings

For staffing, a clock-face eight-hour overnight shift may be nine elapsed hours when it spans the fold. The calculator distinguishes start/end instants and reports physical elapsed time.

Wage entitlement, public-holiday pay and overtime calculations depend on jurisdiction and contract. The clock arithmetic is not a payroll legal conclusion.

Boundary / counterexample: Double execution of scheduled tasks can occur when software interprets a repeated local time twice. Define the recurrence engine’s ambiguity policy and monitor actual execution.
5

Authority versus browser clock data

GOV.UK and the Irish government provide the rule; browser Intl resolves candidate instants from installed timezone data. Law, vendor implementation and user device updates can differ.

The tool searches possible offsets and reports zero, one or two candidates, without silently dropping one. It also displays a fixed UTC−7 option for most of B.C. rather than assuming outdated Vancouver fallback.

Boundary / counterexample: Verify critical transportation or medical appointment times through the actual provider and recheck time rules before October 2027.

A five-step evidence sequence before committing

01. State the exact task

Write what must happen, where, at what local time, and which institution controls the actual result. Separate federal rule, provider notice, school or employer policy and personal choices.

02. Verify the relevant source

Open the primary authority, confirm whether the information concerns 2027 or an earlier year, and record the original URL plus your check date in the local evidence notebook.

03. Model the downside

Enter your own cost, time and buffer assumptions. Inspect the case where a supplier is unavailable or a device timezone database is stale. A precise calculation is not a complete system plan.

04. Compare alternatives

Compare two real, available options against the same criteria. Count transfer constraints, cancellation terms, accessibility, product scope, total cash cost and unverified conditions.

05. Recheck just before action

Save the provider's date-stamped confirmation; revisit volatile services shortly before travel, shipping or scheduled jobs. The hub is not monitoring those updates for you.

Gate against false certainty

If eligibility, operating hours, dated inventory, future law or a transport timetable is missing, display pending rather than an invented estimate. Do not use historical figures as 2027 facts.

Reverification trigger:

Any change to DST legislation, bank-holiday regulation, regional time-zone definitions, provider timetable or local service availability can invalidate a plan even when the original date remains unchanged. Verify the controlling primary source, not a static snippet.

05 / Questions with boundaries

Answers that do not overclaim

When do UK clocks go back in 2027?

Sunday October 31, 2027, at 02:00 BST to 01:00 GMT.

Why is 01:30 ambiguous?

It occurs once at BST UTC+1 and again at GMT UTC+0.

Does the US change the same night?

No. Most observing US regions change November 7, 2027.

Are train departure times automatically corrected?

Do not assume an operator’s handling; refer to its published timetable and zone notation.
