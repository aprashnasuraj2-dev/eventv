How to interpret a range

On 10 July, the published elite ranges for high diving, swimming and artistic swimming overlap, and the Masters diving range ends that day. The lookup reports all four ranges. That is a calendar overlap only: the source does not establish that each sport has a public session at a particular hour, that the sessions can be combined, or that a single ticket covers them.

On 9 July the published elite water polo and Masters swimming ranges reach their end dates. An inclusive range includes that date, but a competition’s exact programme can still specify which matches or events occur and whether they are open to the public. Recheck the latest official event-by-event schedule when planning.

The elite swimming window begins 10 July while the Masters swimming window runs 3–9 July in the cited announcement. Confusing those two labels could shift a trip by a week. The table and tool retain the championship level on every result to help avoid that mistake.

Venue and ticket checks

World Aquatics says the main elite championships venues are MVM Dome for swimming and water polo, and Duna Arena for diving and artistic swimming. Its venue announcement said the high-diving and open-water venues would be confirmed later. Do not infer those locations from the other four sports, and do not automatically apply elite venue assignments to the separate Masters competition.

When actual session and ticket information is available, compare the event, championship level, venue, date, session, seat category and entry conditions. A ticket for one sport or venue is not automatically valid for another. Check current prices, availability, refund terms and accessible seating with the official organiser.

For two hypothetical sessions ending at 14:00 and starting at 15:30, a user-entered 60-minute transfer produces a 30-minute margin. A 100-minute transfer produces a ten-minute shortfall. Neither result accounts for a late finish, transport disruption, security, inaccessible paths or a queue unless the visitor includes those in the allowance.

Plan across Budapest responsibly

  1. Confirm the latest elite or Masters programme and individual session times; the broad windows above are not a live schedule.
  2. Verify the exact venue and ticket for each session, including accessible seating and companion arrangements.
  3. Check Budapest transport operators for current travel routes, accessibility and service changes, and allow time for security and queues.
  4. For international travel, consult the responsible Hungarian and Schengen authorities about the requirements for your own passport and itinerary.
  5. Recheck weather and official guidance for open-water and outdoor events; a published range does not promise unchanged conditions.
