    Before booking: practical checks

    Ticket evidence

    Verify the exact date, session, venue and seating/access category against an official seller. A ticket to one session does not necessarily cover another.

    Travel and accessibility

    Check entry documents with your relevant consulate, and verify venue step-free access, transit changes and entry gates directly. Nationality-specific visa rules cannot be inferred here.

    Qualification is not a start list

    World Athletics has published a qualification framework, but an athlete meeting a standard is not proof of a final entry or scheduled appearance.

    Questions with evidence boundaries

    Is 11 September still the opening date?

    No. The organizer explicitly revised the dates to 10–19 September 2027. Older 11–19 notices are historical, not the current schedule.

    Are the times in this planner official?

    No. Every entered row is hypothetical. The final session timetable was pending approval in the cited organizer announcement.

    Does a valid itinerary mean I can attend?

    No. A non-overlapping plan does not establish ticket inventory, entry permission, visa status, seat access or transport availability.

    Can I use this across timezones?

    Yes. Enter Beijing-local scenarios, then switch display timezone. London and New York daylight-saving offsets are calculated for the selected dates.
