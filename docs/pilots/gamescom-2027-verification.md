# Gamescom 2027 — editorial verification
Verified: 2026-11-05

## Primary facts used
- Official gamescom visitor/home pages state gamescom 2027 runs August 23–29 in Cologne.
- Official visitor/home pages: gamescom dev begins the week on Aug 23 and 24; Opening Night Live is the evening of Aug 24; Aug 25 is Business & Media Day; general public opening starts Aug 26.
- Official private-visitor opening hours:
  - Wed Aug 25: 13:00–19:00, limited Wildcard private access during Business & Media Day.
  - Thu Aug 26: 10:00–20:00.
  - Fri Aug 27: 10:00–20:00.
  - Sat Aug 28: 09:00–20:00.
  - Sun Aug 29: 09:00–20:00.
- Current trade-visitor hours differ and the business area closes after Friday.
- Official Gamescom ticket page is currently promoting notification for the opening of the 2027 ticket shop; no private-ticket price is asserted here.
- Koelnmesse official address: Messeplatz 1, 50679 Cologne, Germany.
- Current Gamescom arrival guidance says Köln Messe/Deutz is accessible by S-Bahn, Regionalbahn, RegionalExpress and long-distance rail; halls are within walking distance via South Entrance. Current guidance also highlights KVB lines 3 and 4.
- Current private-visitor opening page says under-4s are not admitted; ages 4–6 enter free; ages 4–12 must be accompanied; ages 12+ may attend without adult supervision.

## Official-source inconsistency deliberately surfaced
- Gamescom visitor/home pages currently describe gamescom dev as Aug 23–24.
- Gamescom trade-visitor page currently says gamescom dev takes place Aug 23–25.
- Because official pages disagree, this EventV page does not use a precise gamescom dev end date for booking guidance and tells readers to verify the dedicated dev site.

## Deliberately not asserted
- 2027 private-visitor ticket prices.
- Exact Opening Night Live 2027 show time.
- Final exhibitors, games, stage program or hall allocation.
- 2027 public-transport disruptions, shuttle buses or construction.
- A definitive gamescom dev end date while official pages conflict.
- Queue times.
- Live hotel, rail, air or food prices.

## Tools and limitations
- Day planner uses official public opening windows but all stop/queue/walking durations are user estimates.
- Budget tool is arithmetic from user-entered prices and has no booking feed.
- Stable venue/station access guidance is included; 2026 disruption notices are intentionally not reused as 2027 facts.

## Deepening pass — 2026-11-05

Additional official Gamescom material was reviewed to make the page useful for multi-day and accessibility planning.

### Accessibility
Official Gamescom accessibility guidance currently says:
- some areas of the venue are not fully accessible;
- information staff can help visitors find suitable routes and can accompany visitors if needed;
- accessible toilets are available in every hall;
- most elevators are accessible and shown on the hall plan;
- Entrance North is recommended for wheelchair users and is served by taxis;
- visitors arriving by public transport are advised to use Entrance South or Entrance East;
- special accessible parking is available at Entrances North, South and East.

The page does not reuse exact 2026 hall positions as 2027 positions. The official hall-plan page currently labels the displayed allocation as 2026.

### Visitor-mode logic
The page now distinguishes:
- private/public visitors: Aug 26–29;
- limited Wildcard private access on Aug 25 from 13:00;
- trade visitors: 09:00 access with business/entertainment-area hours varying by day; business area closed after Friday.

### Multi-day capacity
The new multi-day calculator uses only the official private/Wildcard opening windows and subtracts a user-defined daily buffer. It does not predict queue times.
