# Steam Next Fest February 2027 — editorial verification
Verified: 2026-11-05

## Primary facts used
- Steam Next Fest February 2027: Feb 22, 2027 at 10:00 AM PST (UTC-8) through Mar 1, 2027 at 10:00 AM PST.
- Steam describes Next Fest as a multi-day/weeklong event for upcoming games with playable demos, developer interaction and livestreams.
- Current Steamworks eligibility says a title can participate in only one Next Fest and must have a public playable demo for the event.
- Steamworks says most sections are broadly randomized in the first few days, with logged-in recommendations becoming more personalized from event behavior after several days.
- Feb 11 press preview, Feb 22 event start, Mar 1 end and wrap-up are in Valve's edition-specific documentation.
- June 14–21, 2027 is the next currently listed 2027 Next Fest.

## Deliberately not asserted
- Final February 2027 participating-game lineup.
- "Best" demos before hands-on editorial testing.
- Guaranteed demo availability after the event.
- Exact player-facing category layout for the February edition.
- Any forecast of most-played titles.
