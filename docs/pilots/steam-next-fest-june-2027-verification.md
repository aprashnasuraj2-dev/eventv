# Steam Next Fest June 2027 — editorial verification
Verified: 2026-11-05

## Primary facts used
- Steam Next Fest June 2027 runs June 14 at 10:00 AM PDT (UTC-7) through June 21 at 10:00 AM PDT.
- Valve describes Next Fest as a weeklong/multi-day event for upcoming unreleased games with playable demos, developer interaction and livestreams.
- June 3, 2027 at 10:00 AM PDT is the currently listed Press Preview start.
- April 25 is the registration deadline; May 3 trailer pull; May 17 press-preview review target; May 31 required-items review deadline in the current official June edition page.
- June 21 includes the wrap-up page featuring the most-played demos.
- A game may participate in only one Next Fest.
- Valve says most Next Fest areas are broadly randomized during the first few days, then logged-in recommendations can become more personalized from behavior during the event.
- Valve tells developers to communicate when a demo will be deactivated after Next Fest, so post-event demo availability is title-specific.
- The next edition after June is planned for October 2027; exact October dates are not asserted here.

## Deliberately not asserted
- Final June 2027 participating-game lineup.
- "Best demos" before hands-on editorial testing.
- Per-title demo duration.
- Steam Deck status for a specific title without a source.
- Whether an individual demo remains playable after June 21.
- Exact October 2027 dates.
