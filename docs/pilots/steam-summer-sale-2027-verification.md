# Steam Summer Sale 2027 — editorial verification
Verified: 2026-11-05

## Primary facts used
- Valve Steamworks currently lists Steam Summer Sale 2027 for June 24 through July 8, 2027.
- Valve says Steam runs four Steam-wide seasonal sales each year.
- Spring Sale 2027 is listed for March 18–25; Autumn Sale 2027 remains TBD in the current calendar.
- The official Summer Sale 2027 source reviewed for this page does not publish an exact start/end clock. Therefore the page shows exact clock as TBA and does not infer 10 a.m. Pacific from other Steam promotion types.
- Valve says seasonal-sale front-page options refresh regularly throughout the sale.
- Steam seasonal sales are exempt from the ordinary 30-day discount cooldown, but release and price-increase cooldowns still apply.
- Steamworks currently describes standard discount thresholds of 10%–95% and says discounts at or above 20% can automatically trigger wishlist notifications when the documented conditions are satisfied.
- Wishlist notification criteria used here: 20%+, duration over 8 hours, and the lowest-priced package must also be discounted.
- Steam’s standard game/software refund offer generally covers requests within 14 days of purchase and with less than two hours of playtime; Steam states it may review requests outside those rules and that some jurisdictions have additional rights.
- Steam explicitly says it does not consider it refund abuse to refund a title bought just before a sale and immediately rebuy it for the sale price, subject to the refund process/policy.

## Deliberately not asserted
- Exact Summer Sale 2027 start/end time.
- Specific game discounts or participation.
- Historical-low claims.
- Live store prices, taxes, regional checkout totals or bundle value.
- Autumn Sale 2027 dates.
- Guaranteed wishlist email/mobile delivery.
- Guaranteed refund eligibility or approval.

## Tools and limitations
- Money + playtime capacity is arithmetic from user-entered assumptions, not a recommendation.
- Cart planner uses manually entered price/discount/hour estimates and has no live Steam price feed.
- Wishlist checker evaluates the documented developer-side thresholds only; user settings and Steam delivery systems still matter.
- Refund precheck compares current date/playtime to the standard 14-day / under-2-hours baseline only. It is not Steam Support.
