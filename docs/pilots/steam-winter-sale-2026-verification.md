# Steam Winter Sale 2026 — editorial verification
Verified: 2026-11-05

## Primary facts used
- Steam Winter Sale 2026 runs December 17, 2026 through January 4, 2027 at 10:00 AM Pacific.
- Valve describes the event as shopping for games and voting for favorites in the 2026 Steam Awards.
- Steam runs four broad seasonal sales; Winter, Spring and Summer 2027 dates are already published.
- Valve says seasonal-sale front-page options refresh regularly through the sale to distribute visibility.
- Steam seasonal-sale discounts are exempt from the normal 30-day discount cooldown, but release and price-increase cooldowns still apply.
- Valve's current discount documentation says ordinary discounts range from 10% to 95%; 20% or greater can automatically trigger wishlist notifications.
- Steam's consumer wishlist page confirms sale notifications can be sent by email or mobile app, subject to user settings.

## Deliberately not asserted
- Any specific Winter Sale 2026 game discount.
- Historical-low or "best deal" claims without independent historical data.
- Final Steam Awards ballot/categories or voting deadline beyond Valve's general statement that voting is part of the sale.
- A particular game's participation.
- Region-specific checkout taxes or final prices.
