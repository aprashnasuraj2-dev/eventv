# Tour de France UK Grand Départ 2027 — editorial verification
Verified: 2026-11-05

## Core confirmed route
- Men’s British Grand Départ: Friday 2 July through Sunday 4 July 2027.
- Stage 1: Edinburgh -> Carlisle.
- Stage 2: Keswick -> Liverpool.
- Stage 3: Welshpool -> Cardiff.
- Great Britain hosts Scotland, England and Wales across the three stages.
- The 2027 Tour begins in Edinburgh; this is the northernmost Grand Départ/start in Tour history according to current Tour material.

## Stage anchors used
### Stage 1
- GB route page: 184 km.
- letour.fr overall route: 185 km.
- Edinburgh -> Midlothian -> Scottish Borders -> Liddesdale -> brief Dumfries & Galloway -> Cumberland -> Carlisle.
- Sole categorized climb on GB route page: Côte de Melrose.
- Intermediate sprint: Newcastleton.
- Finish outside Carlisle's medieval castle.
- Edinburgh Council route begins at Palace of Holyroodhouse and names Royal Mile, North Bridge, Princes Street, The Mound, Castle area, Grassmarket and other streets before leaving the city.

### Stage 2
- 223 km.
- Starts Keswick; passes Thirlmere, Grasmere, Rydal Water, Windermere and Morecambe Bay corridor.
- Intermediate sprint: Morecambe.
- Côte de Jubilee Tower plus four further categorized climbs on current GB route page.
- Finish on The Strand in Liverpool.

### Stage 3
- 223 km.
- Welshpool -> Cardiff.
- Intermediate sprint: Llandrindod Wells.
- GB route page says eight categorized climbs, six in final 80 km, including Côte de Rhigos and Côte de Caerffili.
- Tour de France commentary page currently says seven Welsh climbs. This source conflict is surfaced rather than resolved editorially.

## Spectator/road-closure facts
- Grand Départ GB resident information describes the event as a free-to-attend, family-friendly spectacle.
- The publicity caravan precedes the race; official resident information describes a two-hour partner show but does not establish one universal fixed caravan-to-peloton gap for every route point.
- Full road closures will be required along the stages.
- Diversions and temporary traffic management will be used.
- Exact road closure/reopening timing, parking and accessible viewing are treated as local operational facts, not generalized from rider passage time.
- Edinburgh Council says detailed city service/closure planning is ongoing and will be updated nearer the event.

## Opening ceremony / presentation
- Tour key dates list team presentation at Edinburgh Castle on Wednesday 30 June.
- Current Grand Départ GB ticket article says tickets started from £27 and its listed sale windows are sold out.
- The article also says celebrations extend beyond the Castle and riders can be seen en route; the race itself remains described by resident material as free-to-attend.

## Deliberate source conflicts preserved
1. Stage 1 distance: 184 km on official GB route page vs 185 km on letour.fr overall route.
2. Stage 3 categorized climbs: 8 on official GB route page vs 7 in a Tour de France commentary page.

## Deliberately not asserted
- Exact rider pass time for arbitrary roadside locations.
- Exact local closure/reopening times everywhere.
- Universal parking availability.
- Universal accessible viewing arrangements.
- Weather/crowd density.
- A fixed publicity-caravan lead time before riders.
- Ability to drive between multiple viewing locations on the same stage.

## Structured data decision
The page uses WebPage, Article, ItemList and BreadcrumbList. It intentionally does not use Google Event rich-result markup because this is a multi-location road race across hundreds of kilometres, with no single event venue/address and without one universal spectator start time.
