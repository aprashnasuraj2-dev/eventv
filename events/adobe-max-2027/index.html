Why this page is different

For a creative professional, “what shipped?” matters more than “what was shown?”

MAX can mix keynote announcements, product demos, Sneaks experiments, preview/beta features, general availability, plan changes and regional rollouts. The useful planning job is to record maturity, product, platform, plan, region, usage/credit impact, source and workflow consequence separately.

Demo ≠ GAA keynote or Sneaks demo can be exploratory, preview-only or future-facing.
GA ≠ available to youPlan tier, geography, desktop/web/mobile support, admin controls or usage credits can still constrain access.
Plan inclusion ≠ zero marginal costUsage credits, premium model limits, storage or enterprise licensing can change the effective cost.
Historical conference format ≠ 2027 format2026 had three in-person conference days and two online days. 2027 remains unannounced.

Counterexample: a feature can be “announced at MAX” and still have zero immediate production value if it is a limited preview in the wrong app, region or plan. Conversely, a quiet GA workflow improvement can save hours every week.
