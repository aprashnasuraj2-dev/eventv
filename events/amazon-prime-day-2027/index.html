Market pricing rules

A “Prime Day discount” still lives inside local pricing law.

US

FTC deceptive-pricing guidance rejects artificial former/reference prices. This hub does not treat Amazon’s displayed percentage as independent proof of value.

UK

CMA guidance requires unavoidable charges, including mandatory delivery where applicable, in the total price up front.

GERMANY / EU

Announced reductions use the lowest-price reference from at least the previous 30 days. A 365-day shopper history is additional evidence, not a replacement for that legal reference.

CANADA

Competition Bureau ordinary-selling-price rules use volume/time tests. A “regular price” must be genuine rather than invented to create a discount.

AUSTRALIA

ACCC pricing guidance requires clear total prices and warns against misleading “was/now” or RRP comparisons.

Freshness contract

What must change after Amazon actually announces 2027.

  1. Date: populate start/end only from Amazon-owned marketplace evidence.
  2. Country scope: do not assume all five target markets share U.S. dates.
  3. Membership: re-check fees, free-trial eligibility and whether deal access requires paid or trial membership.
  4. Price: record exact SKU/seller/condition and compare against dated history.
  5. Returns: save item-level return eligibility and exceptions before purchase.
  6. Delivery: compare mandatory delivery and promise wording, not marketing badges.
  7. Alternatives: compare landed cost outside Amazon; do not rank products by affiliate economics.

No live Amazon price, inventory, coupon, membership-status, return-policy, delivery, seller-rating or deal feed is active. All tools use reader-entered values and locally stored evidence.
