The most important fact is the variation.

Official calendars show meaningful differences even inside one country. GOV.UK explicitly says term dates vary across the UK; individual councils and schools can differ. NSW and Queensland publish separate Australian calendars with the school year beginning in January/February, not the Northern Hemisphere late-summer pattern.

Decision rule

School-specific requirements beat generic shopping lists.

A retailer’s “grade 7 essentials” page can be useful inspiration, but it is not your school’s requirement. The safest sequence is: confirm the first day → obtain the school/course list → mark what you already own → verify exact technology requirements → record prices → buy only what remains.

Required ≠ suggestedKeep teacher/school-required items separate from retailer recommendations and household preferences.
Reusable ≠ freeAn existing item saves cash only if it still fits, works and meets the current requirement.
Discount ≠ lowest priceTrack the same model/size/seller and total checkout cost over time.
Student label ≠ compatibilityA laptop can be marketed to students yet fail a course’s required OS, RAM, storage or application support.

Counterexample: a family buys a discounted Chromebook in July, then learns in August that the course requires locally installed software not supported by that device. A lower price produced a higher replacement cost.

Interactive tool / 04 · Texas-only verified preset

Texas 2027 back-to-school sales-tax holiday checker

The Texas Comptroller has already published the Aug 6–8, 2027 holiday. Most qualifying clothing/footwear, specific school supplies and student backpacks must be sold for less than $100 per eligible item. Computers, software and textbooks do not qualify. Seller-added delivery/handling charges count toward the item’s sales price.

Texas-only rule check. Do not use this result for another state or country.
Important: “under $100” means the eligible item’s sales price remains below $100 after seller-added shipping/handling allocated to that item. A $95 pair of jeans plus $10 delivery becomes $105 and does not qualify under the Comptroller’s example. Business-account school-supply purchases can require an exemption certificate.

FAQ / no fabricated national calendar

Back to School 2027 questions

When is Back to School 2027?

There is no universal date. The July 1–September 30 window on this EventV route is an editorial planning season. Actual school starts vary by district, council, school and country.

Do all U.S. schools start in August?

No. District calendars differ and some systems start in September. Even within one district, charter or alternative calendars can differ. Use the student’s official school calendar.

Do UK schools all return on the same day?

No. GOV.UK explicitly says school term and holiday dates vary across the UK. Council examples also differ, and academies/other schools may set their own dates.

Is July–September “back to school” in Australia?

Not in the same annual-school-year sense. NSW and Queensland begin their school years in January/February. July is a Term 3 return after winter holidays.

Is there a national U.S. back-to-school tax-free weekend?

No. Sales-tax holidays are jurisdiction-specific. This page includes a verified Texas 2027 preset only; do not apply it to another state.

Does a Texas laptop qualify because it is for school?

No. The Texas Comptroller’s 2027 back-to-school holiday page explicitly lists computers, software and textbooks among items that do not qualify.

Should I buy refurbished technology for school?

That depends on the exact device condition, warranty, battery/repair risk, return policy and whether it meets the school’s requirements. Compare like-for-like total cost and verify compatibility first.
