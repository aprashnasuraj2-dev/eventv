01 / Evidence first

What is established — and what isn’t

Every confirmed claim below has a relevant authority in the reference list. Dates can be established without proving a future business opening schedule, travel service or personal eligibility. Your own evidence, particularly around 2027 rules, should be rechecked before any financial or logistical commitment.

UK 2027 bank dates

GOV.UK lists Good Friday March 26 across the UK, Easter Monday March 29 in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, not its Scotland-wide bank list.

School dates

GOV.UK says school terms differ by locality; Edinburgh pupils may not begin spring holidays until April 5 in 2027.

Easter Sunday

March 28 is the 2027 Western Easter Sunday; it is not automatically a public holiday with identical rights in every country.

Not all traditions

Eastern Orthodox observance may differ; this hub is explicitly scoped to the Western Easter calendar.

Counterexample that changes the decision:

A Scottish local authority may have an Easter Monday public holiday even though it is not on Scotland's national GOV.UK bank-holiday listing; both statements can be true.

Confirmed / rule-derived

The calendar statements and published statutory time rules above are supported by the identified primary authorities as of the editorial check date. All calculations label their assumptions.

Pending / individual

2027 branch opening times, transit services, travel disruptions, seats, merchandise offers, accommodation and individual eligibility remain unknown unless separately verified from the controlling provider.

04 / Detailed decision playbook

Five practical failure modes — and how to avoid them

1

Four UK calendars, not one rule

England and Wales list Good Friday March 26 and Easter Monday March 29 among bank holidays in 2027. Northern Ireland also lists both. Scotland’s national bank-holiday list includes Good Friday, not Easter Monday.

Do not infer that a store must close, that an employer must provide paid leave, or that every school is shut for precisely four days. GOV.UK explicitly notes that an employer need not grant paid leave on bank holidays.

Boundary / counterexample: For Scottish plans, verify the council, school, or employer’s own calendar. Edinburgh’s 2027 school spring break falls later than Easter weekend, providing a direct counterexample to universal school-break assumptions.
2

School-break mismatches

Use the relevant local education authority or school rather than interpolating from the bank calendar. Edinburgh council lists Good Friday and Easter Monday then pupils returning March 30, with spring holidays April 5–19.

For multi-family travel, collect all relevant school calendars including INSET/staff training days and dates for academies. The earliest start and latest return drive possible travel windows.

Boundary / counterexample: Do not use an entire country as a school calendar. Even schools inside one local authority may specify exceptions.
3

A useful transport comparison

Separate scheduled services, change notices, actual seat/vehicle inventory and disruption alerts. The bank-holiday calendar is evidence of date only; it is not a railway, bus, airline or ferry timetable.

Build an itinerary with reversible booking, plan the last-mile connection, and allow a transfer buffer based on published service constraints once available. Do not output made-up 2027 service frequencies.

Boundary / counterexample: A Sunday timetable is not guaranteed on Good Friday or Easter Monday; each operator may publish a special plan.
4

Meal planning under delivery uncertainty

Meal cost is guests × per-person estimate plus shared supplies and a locally entered contingency. A midweek purchase plan may improve reliability when a shop’s actual Easter opening is unknown.

Dietary needs, refrigerated storage, oven capacity and food safety may matter more than nominal ingredient cost. Evaluate substitution options and delivery constraints before committing.

Boundary / counterexample: A supermarket discount from a previous year is historical and does not establish a 2027 promotion.
5

Daylight saving collision

Sunday March 28 is also when the UK changes to British Summer Time. At 01:00 GMT the clock advances to 02:00 BST. An apparent overnight itinerary containing '01:30 local' may be impossible in London.

The browser local-time resolver in the UK clocks-forward hub can reveal this gap, while named-zone conversion prevents assuming a constant UK-US offset. Some regions do not change clocks.

Boundary / counterexample: Easter Sunday and a clock transition are independent events; a bank-holiday table alone cannot settle a transport timestamp across the change.

A five-step evidence sequence before committing

01. State the exact task

Write what must happen, where, at what local time, and which institution controls the actual result. Separate federal rule, provider notice, school or employer policy and personal choices.

02. Verify the relevant source

Open the primary authority, confirm whether the information concerns 2027 or an earlier year, and record the original URL plus your check date in the local evidence notebook.

03. Model the downside

Enter your own cost, time and buffer assumptions. Inspect the case where a supplier is unavailable or a device timezone database is stale. A precise calculation is not a complete system plan.

04. Compare alternatives

Compare two real, available options against the same criteria. Count transfer constraints, cancellation terms, accessibility, product scope, total cash cost and unverified conditions.

05. Recheck just before action

Save the provider's date-stamped confirmation; revisit volatile services shortly before travel, shipping or scheduled jobs. The hub is not monitoring those updates for you.

Gate against false certainty

If eligibility, operating hours, dated inventory, future law or a transport timetable is missing, display pending rather than an invented estimate. Do not use historical figures as 2027 facts.

Reverification trigger:

Any change to DST legislation, bank-holiday regulation, regional time-zone definitions, provider timetable or local service availability can invalidate a plan even when the original date remains unchanged. Verify the controlling primary source, not a static snippet.

05 / Questions with boundaries

Answers that do not overclaim

Is Easter 2027 on March 28?

Western Easter Sunday falls on March 28, 2027.

Is Easter Monday a UK-wide bank holiday?

No: it appears in England/Wales and Northern Ireland national lists, not Scotland’s GOV.UK bank-holiday list.

Will every UK school be off that week?

No. Council and school dates vary.

Can a train timetable be inferred from bank holidays?

No; consult the operator’s live or officially published timetable for the journey.
