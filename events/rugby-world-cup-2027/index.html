The planning problem

“7pm in Australia” is not one timezone—and “if my team wins” is not one itinerary.

Perth, Brisbane/Townsville, Adelaide and the southeastern cities do not share one UTC offset through October and November. The official schedule wisely publishes local venue time; a fan planner has to preserve each city’s real timezone before converting for London, Dublin, Paris, Auckland or anywhere else.

Fixture certaintyPool matches are fixed by team. Knockout slots are fixed by pool finish or prior-match winner—not by today’s prediction.
Timezone correctnessUse the venue’s IANA timezone for the actual date. Do not apply one “Australia time” offset to the whole tournament.
Travel-buffer honestyHours between kickoffs are computable. Flight, rail, road, security and airport transfer times are not assumed by this page.
Ticket-state freshness“Tickets on sale now” is a current official status. Match/category availability still has to be checked on the official ticketing site.

Counterexample: a supporter can have more than 24 elapsed hours between two kickoffs and still have a fragile itinerary if those games are in Sydney and Perth. Time gap ≠ transport feasibility.

New 24-team format

Why the knockout path is different in 2027

Australia 2027 is the first Men’s Rugby World Cup with 24 teams. Six pools of four produce 36 pool matches. The top two from each pool advance, joined by the four best third-place teams, creating eight Round-of-16 matches before the quarter-finals.

FAQ / source-sensitive answers

Rugby World Cup 2027 planning questions

When is Rugby World Cup 2027?

Men’s Rugby World Cup 2027 runs from Friday, October 1 through Saturday, November 13, 2027 in Australia.

Where is the opening match?

Australia v Hong Kong China at Perth Stadium in Perth | Boorloo on October 1 at 18:45 local time.

When and where is the final?

Saturday, November 13 at Stadium Australia in Sydney | Wangal, with the official schedule listing a 20:00 local kickoff.

Are all kickoff times confirmed?

Yes. Rugby World Cup’s official schedule says dates, venues and kickoff times are confirmed for all 52 matches. Stadium gate-opening times are a different question and remain to be published closer to the tournament.

Are Rugby World Cup 2027 tickets on sale?

The official main RWC 2027 site currently says tickets are on sale now. Availability varies by match/category and is not mirrored on this page.

Can I know where my team will play in the Round of 16?

If the team finishes first or second in its pool, the official bracket identifies the Round-of-16 slot and venue. If it qualifies as one of the four best third-place teams, the exact slot depends on the final combination of qualifying third-place pools.

Can I book a cross-country flight immediately after a match based on this tool?

No. The itinerary tool only calculates time between kickoffs. Add realistic post-match exit, ground transport, airport check-in, flight time, delay and arrival-city transport before committing.
