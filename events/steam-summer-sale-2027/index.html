Why some games may be absent

A Steam-wide sale is broad, but eligibility still has constraints

30 DAYS

Recent releases

Valve says products generally cannot be discounted within 30 days of release, except a configured launch discount. The seasonal-sale exception does not erase that release cooldown.

30 DAYS

Recent price increases

A product cannot be discounted for 30 days after a base-price increase in any currency. Seasonal sales do not override that price-increase cooldown.

20%+

Wishlist notification threshold

Steamworks says discounts at or above 20% can trigger wishlist notifications when the other notification criteria are satisfied.

REFRESH

Store featuring changes

Valve says seasonal-sale featuring refreshes regularly across the event rather than locking every featured position to a single day.

FAQ

Steam Summer Sale 2027 questions

When is Steam Summer Sale 2027?

Valve currently lists the sale for June 24 through July 8, 2027.

What time does Steam Summer Sale 2027 start?

The exact start clock is not stated in the official 2027 Summer Sale source reviewed for this page. We will add local-time conversions after Valve publishes a specific time.

Will every Steam game be discounted?

No. Steam invites released games to seasonal sales, but individual participation, cooldown eligibility and discount size still vary by publisher/developer and product.

Does a 20% discount trigger a Steam wishlist alert?

Steamworks says discounts at or above 20% can trigger wishlist notifications when the lowest-priced package is included and the discount lasts more than eight hours. Actual delivery is not guaranteed for every user.

What is Steam’s standard game refund rule?

Steam says game/software refund requests are generally eligible under its standard offer when made within 14 days of purchase and with less than two hours of playtime. Exceptions and additional jurisdictional rights can apply.

Can I refund a game bought just before the sale and rebuy it cheaper?

Steam’s refund policy says it does not consider it abuse to request a refund for a title purchased just before a sale and then immediately rebuy it for the sale price, assuming the refund request is handled under Steam’s policy.

Why might a recently released game skip the Summer Sale?

Steam’s discount rules retain a 30-day release cooldown and 30-day price-increase cooldown even though seasonal sales are exempt from the normal discount cooldown.
