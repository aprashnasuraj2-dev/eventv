What this page solves

Summer Game Fest is an ecosystem of showcases, not one timestamp.

In 2026, the flagship Summer Game Fest show happened on June 5, but the official SGF directory contained surrounding partner, community and flagship events across multiple days. That means the practical planning problem is not “what time is SGF?” but which official showcase, which source, which timezone, and what overlaps with what?

Main show ≠ whole scheduleA single flagship date does not define every partner or platform showcase in the SGF event directory.
Official announcement ≠ game releaseA reveal can confirm a trailer, platform or window without confirming a final release date.
Publisher-confirmed ≠ rumorKeep first-party statements separate from press reports, leaks, ratings-board entries and social speculation.
Replay ≠ rebroadcast rightsAn official YouTube or Twitch replay is evidence of a legal viewing source; that does not automatically grant co-streaming or reuse rights.

Counterexample: in 2026, the flagship SGF show was June 5, while the official SGF directory included The Mix on June 1 and Nintendo Direct on June 9. Therefore “SGF 2027 is one weekend” is not a safe schedule assumption.
