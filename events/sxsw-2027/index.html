The decision model

Plan SXSW as a capacity-constrained network, not a list of bookmarks.

A useful itinerary has four independent constraints: time overlap, venue transfer, credential scope, and admission uncertainty. Solving only the calendar can still produce an impossible day.

Badge ≠ guaranteed session entrySXSW’s credential terms explicitly say access is limited by venue capacity, restrictions and other event-specific constraints.
Reservation ≠ every seatReservations apply only to certain events and reserve only a portion of the room; walk-up lines remain part of the system.
Small gap ≠ feasible venue transferA 15-minute gap is only useful if your actual exit, walking/shuttle, queue and entry buffer fit inside it.
“Downtown” ≠ one buildingThe official footprint spans downtown, locations south of Lady Bird Lake, and a Film venue in North-Central Austin.

Counterexample: two sessions can be non-overlapping on paper and still be mutually exclusive in practice if the first runs late, the next venue is far away, the shuttle is not immediate, or admission closes at capacity. The planner below therefore never auto-invents transfer minutes.

What do you need your badge to explicitly include?

All official SXSW badges also list a universal experience that includes Keynotes, Featured Sessions, Comedy Festival showcases, Podcast Festival, SXSW Expo, shuttle service and more. This tool does not convert “not explicitly included” into “definitely impossible”; access can be event-specific.

Credential rule: SXSW says a credential allows access to defined events but does not guarantee admission to every element. Venue capacity, age rules, technical limits and venue policies can still block entry.

Reservation rule: a reservation can guarantee access to a participating event, but reservations exist for only some events and represent only part of room capacity.

Access logic

What every official badge gets — and where certainty stops

The current SXSW cart says every official badge includes a universal layer: brand activations and lounges, Congress Avenue Block Party, Comedy Festival showcases, Featured Sessions, Flatstock, Global & Radio Day Music stages, Keynotes, SXSW Bookstore, SXSW EDU crossover days, SXSW Expo and programming, SXSW Shuttle Service, XR Experience and Podcast Festival.

That universal layer does not erase vertical differences. The cart separately lists Innovation Conference for Innovation badges, Film & TV Festival + Conference for Film & TV badges, Music Festival + Conference for Music badges, and all three for Platinum.

Counterexample: buying the most expensive general badge can broaden credential scope but cannot turn a full room into an available seat. Conversely, a cheaper badge can be the more efficient match if your goals stay inside one vertical and you plan realistic transfer and queue buffers.
