Awards process in one page

Nomination, eligibility, fan voting and final winners are four different states.

Most nominees are selected by an international voting jury of more than 100 media and influencer outlets. Specialized juries handle categories including esports, accessibility and best adaptation. Final winners use the published 90% jury / 10% fan blend. The producer and advisory board do not vote on nominees or winners, and the FAQ says game companies are not told winners in advance.

Eligible ≠ nominatedClearing the release cutoff only means a game can be considered; it does not guarantee a nomination.
Nominated ≠ likely winnerNomination count, social volume and fan polls are not the official 90/10 result calculation.
Fan vote ≠ 100%For the main awards, fan voting is 10% of the final blend. Players’ Voice is a separate category.
Announcement ≠ award resultWorld premieres and game trailers during the show should be tracked separately from category winners.

Counterexample: the most popular game in an online poll can lose an award even if it dominates fan sentiment, because public voting is only 10% of the standard winner calculation.
