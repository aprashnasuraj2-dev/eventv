FAQ

Tour de France UK Grand Départ 2027 questions

When is the Tour de France in the UK in 2027?

The men’s Tour de France Grand Départ runs in Great Britain from Friday July 2 through Sunday July 4, 2027.

What are the three UK stages?

Stage 1: Edinburgh to Carlisle. Stage 2: Keswick to Liverpool. Stage 3: Welshpool to Cardiff.

Is it free to watch the Tour de France on the roadside?

The official Grand Départ GB resident page describes the races as a free-to-attend, family-friendly spectacle. Local safety, access and closure restrictions still apply. The separate Edinburgh team-presentation ceremony is ticketed.

Where is the Stage 2 finish in Liverpool?

The official GB route page says the stage finishes on The Strand in Liverpool, under the Three Graces.

Which stage is best for climbs?

Stages 2 and 3 are both hilly. Stage 2 has five categorized climbs according to current official material; Stage 3 has a dense late climbing sequence through South Wales. Official sources currently disagree on whether Stage 3 has seven or eight categorized climbs, so this page does not pretend that count is settled.

Where are the intermediate sprints?

Newcastleton on Stage 1, Morecambe on Stage 2 and Llandrindod Wells on Stage 3.

When will roads close?

Full road closures are confirmed, but use the local authority or Grand Départ update for your exact road and timing. Do not derive closure time from the expected rider pass time.

Can I drive from one viewing point to another during the same stage?

This planner does not recommend assuming you can. Extensive full closures and diversions mean a one-primary-position plan is safer for early planning unless official local access information clearly supports another movement plan.
