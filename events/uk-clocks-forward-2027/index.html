01 / Evidence first

What is established — and what isn’t

Every confirmed claim below has a relevant authority in the reference list. Dates can be established without proving a future business opening schedule, travel service or personal eligibility. Your own evidence, particularly around 2027 rules, should be rechecked before any financial or logistical commitment.

GOV.UK 2027

GOV.UK lists clocks forward March 28, 2027 at 01:00 GMT, to 02:00 BST.

Ireland

The Irish government documents the final-Sunday-in-March rule at 01:00 GMT; check Irish terminology when communicating.

Easter clash

Western Easter Sunday is also March 28, 2027; calendar, transport and religious observance are separate systems.

US mismatch

Observing U.S. time zones changed on March 14; fixed offsets are invalid for the gap fortnight.

Counterexample that changes the decision:

A New York 01:30 on March 28 is a valid local time, while a London 01:30 on that date is nonexistent.

04 / Detailed decision playbook

Five practical failure modes — and how to avoid them

1

The missing hour is specifically 01:00–01:59

GOV.UK’s rule describes clocks moving forward at 01:00 GMT on the last Sunday in March. On March 28, 2027 the UK goes directly to 02:00 BST.

An overnight hotel booking, recurring backup job or scheduled call assigned London 01:30 on that date has no corresponding UTC instant. The resolver explicitly says 'nonexistent' instead of quietly shifting it.

Boundary / counterexample: A valid-looking calendar value can be an invalid local instant; transport or calendar providers must choose their documented normalization policies.
2

Easter Sunday adds practical complexity

The UK clock change occurs on Western Easter Sunday in 2027. Good Friday is March 26 and Easter Monday March 29 in England/Wales and Northern Ireland national bank-holiday schedules.

A holiday can affect whether services run; the clock transition changes what timestamps mean. Neither tells you a specific bus arrival, train departure or store opening time.

Boundary / counterexample: Check each operator’s published Easter schedule and use destination-local zone when interpreting tickets.
3

Ireland follows an equivalent instant

Ireland’s government sets the end of winter time at 01:00 GMT on the final Sunday in March. It transitions simultaneously with the UK, though terms like Irish Standard Time and British Summer Time differ.

Avoid assuming an event is scheduled in UTC merely because a poster says GMT colloquially. Confirm the named zone or UTC offset from the organizer.

Boundary / counterexample: Ireland may use a different legal time vocabulary than the UK; a wall-clock equality is not evidence of identical statute text.
4

US and UK move on different Sundays

Observing U.S. zones advanced on March 14, 2027, while London transitions March 28. During that two-week gap, a New York–London call temporarily shifts its usual time difference.

Use a city’s IANA ID (Europe/London or America/New_York) and the full target date. Static EST/BST abbreviations without a date can be misleading.

Boundary / counterexample: If your team has a permanent time zone like most of B.C., convert from the actual permanent offset instead of using old Vancouver DST assumptions.
5

Check the software, not only the authority

This converter uses the browser’s timezone database and scans candidate UTC offsets for your wall-clock time. It can identify nonexistent or repeated times, with a fixed UTC−7 B.C. comparator.

A device with an outdated timezone database can disagree with published law. For time-sensitive flights, shift work and alarms, confirm firmware/OS updates and the provider’s official schedule.

Boundary / counterexample: No live timezone-law change feed is connected. Dates and rules must be reverified near the actual 2027 travel or meeting.

A five-step evidence sequence before committing

01. State the exact task

Write what must happen, where, at what local time, and which institution controls the actual result. Separate federal rule, provider notice, school or employer policy and personal choices.

02. Verify the relevant source

Open the primary authority, confirm whether the information concerns 2027 or an earlier year, and record the original URL plus your check date in the local evidence notebook.

03. Model the downside

Enter your own cost, time and buffer assumptions. Inspect the case where a supplier is unavailable or a device timezone database is stale. A precise calculation is not a complete system plan.

04. Compare alternatives

Compare two real, available options against the same criteria. Count transfer constraints, cancellation terms, accessibility, product scope, total cash cost and unverified conditions.

05. Recheck just before action

Save the provider's date-stamped confirmation; revisit volatile services shortly before travel, shipping or scheduled jobs. The hub is not monitoring those updates for you.

Gate against false certainty

If eligibility, operating hours, dated inventory, future law or a transport timetable is missing, display pending rather than an invented estimate. Do not use historical figures as 2027 facts.

Reverification trigger:

Any change to DST legislation, bank-holiday regulation, regional time-zone definitions, provider timetable or local service availability can invalidate a plan even when the original date remains unchanged. Verify the controlling primary source, not a static snippet.

05 / Questions with boundaries

Answers that do not overclaim

What happens at 01:00 on March 28, 2027?

UK clocks advance to 02:00 BST, skipping one wall-clock hour.

Is 01:30 a valid London time on that date?

No, not under the currently published rule.

Are Irish clocks different?

Ireland transitions at the same instant under its winter/summer time rules; names and legal framing differ.

Does Easter mean trains will not run?

No such general timetable claim is made; operator-specific schedules are necessary.
