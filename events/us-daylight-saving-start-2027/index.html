01 / Evidence first

What is established — and what isn’t

Every confirmed claim below has a relevant authority in the reference list. Dates can be established without proving a future business opening schedule, travel service or personal eligibility. Your own evidence, particularly around 2027 rules, should be rechecked before any financial or logistical commitment.

United States

NIST identifies second Sunday in March, local 02:00 → 03:00 for observing areas.

Exceptions

U.S. DOT identifies Hawaii and most of Arizona as non-observing; Navajo exceptions need locality-level verification.

Canada

Provincial and territorial law controls. British Columbia adopted permanent UTC−7 Pacific Time in 2026 for most of the province.

UK gap weeks

The UK changes on March 28, 2027, two weeks after the US. Named zones prevent false constant offsets.

Counterexample that changes the decision:

Phoenix never loses 02:30 on March 14; New York does. Vancouver should not be modeled as an ordinary 2027 U.S.-style DST city.

04 / Detailed decision playbook

Five practical failure modes — and how to avoid them

1

Why 02:30 can be an impossible time

The current U.S. rule shifts the clock from 02:00 standard time to 03:00 daylight time on the second Sunday of March. In New York, local March 14 2027 02:30 does not correspond to a real instant.

A booking form that silently normalizes a missing time to 03:30 can create a one-hour error. This resolver searches actual instants for the entered wall-clock time and reports zero, one or two matches; it does not quietly guess.

Boundary / counterexample: Different zones change at different UTC instants, so a meeting across New York, Chicago and London should be anchored to a real UTC instant, not a date plus a memorized offset.
2

Cross-border time laws are not interchangeable

The U.S. DOT is the federal regulator for DST and permits state exemptions. Hawaii and most of Arizona do not observe DST. In Canada, time arrangements depend on province, territory and local exceptions.

British Columbia announced 2026 adoption of permanent year-round Pacific Time (UTC−7) for most communities, with parts of northern and southeastern B.C. continuing separate arrangements. A website repeating 'Vancouver falls back in November' from an old data set would be wrong.

Boundary / counterexample: This tool supplies a manual fixed UTC−7 B.C. choice, separate from IANA zone data that may not yet reflect newly changed legislation in the visitor’s browser.
3

The US–UK two-week trap

Most of the United States advances on March 14, 2027; the UK advances March 28. During that interval the transatlantic difference changes by one hour for affected cities.

A recurring call scheduled only as '9 a.m. Eastern = 2 p.m. London' can be wrong for two weeks. Convert each occurrence from its named origin zone and preserve the date.

Boundary / counterexample: Ireland’s summer-time rules also shift on the final Sunday of March, but Irish legal time terminology differs from U.S. daylight/standard-time vocabulary.
4

Payroll and travel elapsed-time arithmetic

An eight-hour clock-face window spanning the spring transition can represent seven elapsed hours, depending on exact start/end and locality. Employers’ premium, payroll and shift policies vary by jurisdiction and contract.

The shift calculator below uses exact matching candidate instants and counts UTC elapsed time. It does not decide compensable hours, overtime or legal pay entitlement.

Boundary / counterexample: For flights, airline ticket times generally refer to local airport times; match the terminal and flight’s named zone rather than subtracting fixed offsets.
5

Assurance and test cases

Run 2027-03-14 02:30 in America/New_York (zero matches), 2027-03-14 01:30 (one) and 2027-03-14 03:30 (one). Try America/Phoenix at 02:30 as a counterexample.

Timezone rules can change after this article’s review. Browser ICU/IANA zone databases update with software versions; verify critical meetings against the local jurisdiction and service provider at booking time.

Boundary / counterexample: A tool can compute a mathematically correct result from stale timezone data; source verification and client tzdb freshness are therefore separate quality gates.

A five-step evidence sequence before committing

01. State the exact task

Write what must happen, where, at what local time, and which institution controls the actual result. Separate federal rule, provider notice, school or employer policy and personal choices.

02. Verify the relevant source

Open the primary authority, confirm whether the information concerns 2027 or an earlier year, and record the original URL plus your check date in the local evidence notebook.

03. Model the downside

Enter your own cost, time and buffer assumptions. Inspect the case where a supplier is unavailable or a device timezone database is stale. A precise calculation is not a complete system plan.

04. Compare alternatives

Compare two real, available options against the same criteria. Count transfer constraints, cancellation terms, accessibility, product scope, total cash cost and unverified conditions.

05. Recheck just before action

Save the provider's date-stamped confirmation; revisit volatile services shortly before travel, shipping or scheduled jobs. The hub is not monitoring those updates for you.

Gate against false certainty

If eligibility, operating hours, dated inventory, future law or a transport timetable is missing, display pending rather than an invented estimate. Do not use historical figures as 2027 facts.

Reverification trigger:

Any change to DST legislation, bank-holiday regulation, regional time-zone definitions, provider timetable or local service availability can invalidate a plan even when the original date remains unchanged. Verify the controlling primary source, not a static snippet.

05 / Questions with boundaries

Answers that do not overclaim

What time does DST start in New York in 2027?

At local 02:00 on March 14, clocks advance to 03:00 under the current federal rule.

Does Arizona change?

Most of Arizona does not; the Navajo Nation is a notable exception.

Does British Columbia change clocks in 2027?

Most of British Columbia adopted permanent UTC−7 Pacific Time in 2026; some regions have different local arrangements.

Why does my app show a different meeting time?

Check the named zone, full date, browser timezone data version and whether the origin time exists.
