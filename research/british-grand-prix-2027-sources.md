## CONFIRMED
- Formula 1 / FIA 2027 calendar announcement, published 2026-09-16: Silverstone **2–4 July 2027**, **Sprint** round. https://corp.formula1.com/2027-calendar-announced-with-10-sprint-events/
- Official F1 event schedule: Friday 2 July practice and sprint qualifying, Saturday 3 July sprint and qualifying, Sunday 4 July race; session *clock times TBC*. https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2027/great-britain
- F1 ticketing 2027 page identifies Silverstone, Towcester NN12 8TN, and states tickets on sale in the English version. Ticket inventory/prices can change; no static price assumed. https://ticketing.formula1.com/great-britain/
- F1 ticketing identifies advertised rail-station shuttle origins Northampton, Milton Keynes and Coventry. **This does not verify 2027 departure timetables, journey duration, accessibility, capacity or service on a particular ticket.** https://ticketing.formula1.com/great-britain/
- Silverstone 2027 Personal Assistant Scheme page says applications for general admission/grandstand purchasers are due within seven days of purchase and that the form is **available soon**; Blue Badge parking subject to availability, and approval is discretionary. https://www.silverstone.co.uk/events/formula-1-british-grand-prix/tickets/accessibility/application

## PENDING / UNANNOUNCED
- Exact Friday/Saturday/Sunday session times, individual travel timetables, station-to-gate times, road closures, gate hours, parking supply and availability, prices by ticket type, broadcast rights by country and lineup. Do not infer any of these from 2026.
- Ticket-store language variants differ (some show waitlist while English shows on sale): treat availability as dynamic and verify official checkout at purchase time.

## Counterexamples and product boundary
- A 09:20 arrival calculated from user-entered 60-minute ride + 20-minute walk + 30-minute queue is **not** an official shuttle arrival prediction.
- A seven-day PA application deadline is not equivalent to automatic approval or guaranteed Blue Badge parking.
- A Sprint weekend does not mean every ticket includes every session or that a specific session starts at a known time.
- The August 13–15 2027 MotoGP British Grand Prix at Silverstone is a separate event, not the July Formula 1 event: https://www.silverstone.co.uk/events/british-grand-prix-motogp
