# EventV — Top 3 execution briefs and conditional monetization model
Research date 2026-11-05. Planning artifact only. No content deployed. See [CHECKLIST.md](../CHECKLIST.md), [event scorecard](./event-scorecard-2026-11-05.csv), and [model CSV](./impression-scenarios-2026-11-05.csv).

## Interpretation and gating
- **Verified:** Event dates and official calendars, linked below.
- **Estimated:** Western share, peak windows, rankings, query demand, click-through, fill, viewability and CPM. None are measured historical website performance.
- **Unverified:** Search Console country/query baseline for AafnaiPatro, Adsterra actual paid impressions/format caps, live site content quality, live indexation, licensing and sourced price inventory.
- Google Trends is normalized, not exact search volume. Google Search Console **URL SERP impressions** are distinct from sessions, ad requests, filled ads, viewed ads and paid ads.
- Numbers assume organic search visibility has been earned; an unknown new cluster may get zero visits. Discover is unmodeled upside, never a guarantee.
- An editorial article is not automatically eligible for Google Event rich results. Shopping sales and astronomical phenomena should not be presented as attendance events.

## Opportunity-score methodology
S = 0.25*D + 0.15*P + 0.25*C + 0.15*K + 0.20*A
All five sub-scores on 1–10. Higher K means **easier** to compete. Scores are editorial judgments, not independent measurements.
Eclipse = 0.25*8 + 0.15*10 + 0.25*8 + 0.15*6 + 0.20*8 = 8.00.
An otherwise high score does not override YMYL, IP, inadequate lead time or lack of a genuinely useful unique asset.

## Conditional ad model
Ad opportunity math (event month, event's equivalent peak-days only):
Eligible SERP URL impressions/day * attainable CTR = peak-day site sessions.
Modeled **viewable equivalents** = peak sessions/day * equivalent peak-days * pages/session * effectively loaded ad opportunities/page * eligible fill * viewability.
Revenue proxy = viewable equivalents / 1,000 * assumed viewable-effective CPM.
Those are NOT necessarily Adsterra paid impressions. Popunder is event-based and frequency-capped; native/display and Social Bar may have different accounting and CPM vs CPA contracts. Use actual ad network reporting to replace the proxy.
CPM bands **illustrative, not observed Adsterra bids**: eclipse $1–$4; rugby $1–$5; Black Friday $1.50–$6, possibly stronger Q4 purchase intent but no guaranteed premium. A country/device/format mix changes revenue materially. Never use this estimate as guaranteed cash.

*Realistic* means a conditional middle assumption **after** the site earns sufficient eligible SERP impressions; it is not a forecast for the present domain. The real outcome for any new cluster can be zero. Actual paid ads require measurement.

Sensitivities: all else equal, halving attainable CTR halves organic sessions, modeled impressions and revenue. Halving CPM halves revenue but not traffic. Doubling one of two independent multiplicative factors mathematically gives the same 2× revenue; in practice, publisher efforts generally have more control over legitimate relevant traffic than ad auction CPM.
Example middle-model revenues at illustrative CPM midpoints: Eclipse 2797 * $2.50/1000 = $6.99, Rugby 6796 * $3.00/1000 = $20.39, Black Friday 6247 * $3.75/1000 = $23.43. Double traffic ≈ 2× these values, +20% CPM ≈ 1.2×.
Where fill degrades: unsupported browsers/OS, cookie/consent restrictions, capped popunders, weak device/geo bids, ad blockers and slow rendering. Diagnose by country+device+format as ad requests -> valid responses -> filled -> visible -> paid; match GSC clicks, GA4 engagement and Adsterra paid views, but do not equate metrics.

## Repeatable publishing plan
- Reassess slate quarterly; run three clusters maximum.
- T-120: research and facts; T-90: evergreen hub and utility; T-45: spokes indexed; T-14: confirmed-specific updates; event: source-backed timestamped edits; T+1/+7/+30: corrections and postmortem.
- Require author and original utility, clear publication/verified dates, dated sources, and meaningful internal links.
- Watch GSC URL country impressions+CTR, GA4 engaged human sessions, Adsterra valid/paid units, p75 Core Web Vitals and quality complaints.
- Reject build-out when credible expertise, price feeds, data rights, review capacity, or organic search opportunity is absent.
