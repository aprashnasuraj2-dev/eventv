## HISTORICAL

## Counterexample
- A 14:00 meeting ending at 14:45 at one campus and a 15:00 meeting at a different campus cannot be marked feasible solely because the times do not overlap: transfer plus check-in can exceed 15 minutes. Likewise, a valid pass for an exhibition does not automatically confer Ministerial Programme access.
