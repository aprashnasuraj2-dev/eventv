# Topic 100 — Total Solar Eclipse 2027, evidence ledger
Checked 2026-11-10 UTC. **CONFIRMED:** NASA future-eclipses calendar identifies 2027-08-02 as a total solar eclipse with totality in southern Spain, parts of North Africa, Saudi Arabia and Yemen; partial visibility covers a much larger area. https://science.nasa.gov/eclipses/future-eclipses/

**CONFIRMED (calculated prediction, not local guarantee):** NASA GSFC path table gives greatest duration about 6m23s near 26°49′N,31°08′E and says limb corrections can shift path limits by ~1–3 km and duration by ~1–3 seconds. https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/SEpath/SEpath2001/SE2027Aug02Tpath.html

**CONFIRMED independent cross-check:** ESA map identifies totality in southern Spain, northern Africa and Arabian Peninsula, while other areas see only a partial eclipse. https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2026/05/Total_solar_eclipse_2_August_2027_Global_map

**PENDING:** location-specific contact times, cloud forecast, local access restrictions, transport, accommodation and country-specific entry rules; must be checked with authorities before travel. **UNANNOUNCED:** any official EventV viewing event, ticket package or broadcaster. **HISTORICAL:** past eclipse weather does not predict August 2027 sky conditions. **Counterexample:** a viewer in Madrid may see a partial eclipse but should not assume totality merely because Spain is on the totality-country list. **Safety:** Never use the calculator as an eclipse ephemeris or solar-eye-safety clearance. NASA eclipse safety guidance must be followed; optical instruments require proper front-mounted solar filters.
