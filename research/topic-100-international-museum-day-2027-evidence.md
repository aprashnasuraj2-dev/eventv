# Topic #100 — International Museum Day 2027: evidence and release boundaries

Checked 2026-11-10 (UTC+05:45). Editorial replacement for excluded astronomy/eclipses; prior PR #84 closed without merge, history retained.

## CONFIRMED annual framework
ICOM official overview https://icom.museum/en/international-museum-day-2/ states International Museum Day is organised annually on **18 May or around that date**, with local activities potentially lasting a day, weekend or week. This supports a 2027 planning anchor of 18 May, **not** an official list of 2027 participating museums.

## HISTORICAL only
ICOM 2026 theme: Museums Uniting a Divided World, https://icom.museum/en/news/museums-uniting-a-divided-world-the-theme-of-international-museum-day-2026/ . It is **not** a 2027 theme. 2025 theme and materials likewise do not imply 2027 participation.

## UNANNOUNCED / PENDING
2027 theme; participating museums; country/city events; free tickets; venue opening hours; timed admission; accessible services; guided tour languages; ticket booking; local transit and closures. All must be explicitly museum-verified, user-entered, and date-stamped. No universal free-entry or official affiliation claims.

## Counterexample
A museum may hold its celebration on a weekend, close on Tuesday 18 May, require a paid reservation, or offer limited accessible entry. The global observance date alone is **not** sufficient to plan an actual visit.

## Differentiated original tool (implementation pending)
Offline multi-museum visit-feasibility planner: user enters institution, local visit windows, time-zone, travel minutes, timed-entry and access needs, verified evidence URL/date, prices/currency; detect overlaps and insufficient transfers; compare group budget without converting currencies; preserve local data, reset and JSON export. Accessible mobile-first form; no live venue or ticket API.
