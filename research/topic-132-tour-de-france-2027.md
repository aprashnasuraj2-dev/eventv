# Topic #132 — Tour de France 2027 official-source ledger
Checked 2026-11-11 (UTC). Independent guide, not affiliated with A.S.O.

CONFIRMED — Organiser's official route https://www.letour.fr/en/overall-route lists 2–25 July 2027, 21 stages, three rest days, opening Edinburgh–Carlisle on 2 July, and final Paris stage on 25 July. This is the men's Tour, distinct from Tour de France Femmes. City of Edinburgh Council https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/events-venues/tour-de-france-grande-depart-2027 describes the 184 km opening route and says road closure planning is ongoing. UK Sport 22 October 2026 https://www.uksport.gov.uk/news/2026/10/22/tour-de-france confirms the UK stage routes and distinction between men's and women's Grand Départs.

PENDING — Precise roadside passage times, spectator access zones, local closures, accessible viewing and real-time public transport disruption. Do not infer these from a published stage-town itinerary.

COUNTEREXAMPLE — An official stage start town does not mean every road is open or that a visitor can move from the stage start to its finish ahead of the peloton. A separately ticketed Edinburgh Castle opening ceremony is not equivalent to free roadside viewing; check https://www.letourgb.com/opening-ceremony/ for changing ticket status.

HISTORICAL — 2025 host announcement and January 2026 UK host-town announcements preceded the October 2026 full-route reveal; earlier preliminary route descriptions are not the latest stage map.

UNANNOUNCED — Individual stage winners, weather, rider start lists, live arrival times, road-access guarantees. Never manufacture them.
