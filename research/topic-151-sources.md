# Japanese Grand Prix 2027 — official research evidence checked 2026-11-11

- Official calendar: https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2027
- Official ticket and promoter navigation: https://tickets.formula1.com/en

## Confirmed and caveats
- Formula 1's official 2027 calendar lists Suzuka's Japanese Grand Prix for 9–11 April 2027.
- The calendar date alone is not a confirmed session clock time. The converter below deliberately leaves the session start blank and uses the viewer's manually entered time.
- Japan Standard Time (JST) is UTC+9 without seasonal clock changes. Some viewer countries observe daylight saving on these April dates.
- A time conversion cannot establish a broadcaster's territorial rights, ticket access, a live stream or an actual on-track schedule.

## Counterexample
Applying London's winter GMT offset to a Japanese Grand Prix in April can produce a result one hour wrong because London normally observes BST.

## Not established
Exact 2027 session clock time, personal ticket or track-zone entitlement, queue/transport and international broadcasting remain unverified. User-entered calculations are hypothetical; noindex, no editorial sign-off, no release.
