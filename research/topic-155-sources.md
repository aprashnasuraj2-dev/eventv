# Tomorrowland Winter 2027 — official evidence ledger
Checked 2026-11-11. Noindex research draft; independent, not affiliated.

Official sources:
- https://winter.tomorrowland.com/en/welcome/
- https://www.tomorrowland.com/events/

Confirmed / historical / uncertainty boundaries:
- The Tomorrowland organiser announces 20–27 March 2027 for Tomorrowland Winter at Alpe d'Huez.
- Its ticket description explicitly includes a lift and ski pass plus general festival admission.
- Festival packages are described as additionally including accommodation, with different transport add-ons and availability.
- Ticket versus package inclusion must be compared product by product; ski ability, weather and mountain transport are outside the model.

Counterexample: Charging separately for another lift pass when the selected festival ticket already includes that lift/ski access double-counts the entitlement.

Unknown: real-time inventory, future changed terms, individual eligibility, exact service/queue/route conditions. All calculation inputs are user assumptions. No publishing, indexing or browser QA sign-off.
