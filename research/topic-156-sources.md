# Australian Grand Prix 2027 — official evidence ledger
Checked 2026-11-11. Noindex research draft; independent, not affiliated.

Official sources:
- https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2027/australia
- https://ticketing.formula1.com/australia/

Confirmed / historical / uncertainty boundaries:
- Formula 1 confirms 2–4 April 2027 at Albert Park, Melbourne, as the Australian Grand Prix weekend.
- Formula 1 lists a Sprint format: Friday practice and Sprint qualifying, Saturday Sprint and qualifying, and the Sunday race.
- Its official 2027 schedule currently marks actual individual clock times TBC; none are inserted into this calculator.
- A valid race-day seat does not automatically grant entry to another day's Sprint sessions.

Counterexample: A spectator with Sunday-only admission cannot presume Friday Sprint-qualifying access.

Unknown: real-time inventory, future changed terms, individual eligibility, exact service/queue/route conditions. All calculation inputs are user assumptions. No publishing, indexing or browser QA sign-off.
