## CONFIRMED
- Frankfurter Buchmesse publishes the 2027 fair dates as 6–10 October, with Chile named its guest of honour.
- The organiser's 2027 exhibitor year lists early-bird discount end on 30 November 2026 and official registration end on 31 January 2027.
- Custom stand construction plans have an August 1 milestone; the guide separately marks August 31 for furniture and stand-equipment orders.
- Early-bird terms exclude collective exhibitions, so a date alone cannot prove eligibility or establish a price.
