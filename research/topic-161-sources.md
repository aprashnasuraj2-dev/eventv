## CONFIRMED
- The Tokyo Marathon Foundation confirms Sunday 7 March 2027 for the 20th edition and identifies a 40,000-runner field.
- Official entry guidance requires in-person packet pickup before race day with photo ID; it does not offer race-day runner registration.
- General runners, wheelchair athletes, charity participants and other classes have separate conditions; a numeric pace alone does not establish eligibility.
- A dated lottery result or past application window is not an open-entry invitation; the official participant account is authoritative.
