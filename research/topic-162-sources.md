## CONFIRMED / ORGANISER-STATED
- The National Retail Federation confirms its New York Retail's Big Show on 10–12 January 2027 at the Javits Center.
- Its official registration page distinguishes retail-member and nonmember categories with date-dependent fees and separate eligibility conditions.
- An Opening Party is advertised for 9 January; that date is separate from the main 10–12 January conference window.
- Attendees should verify their organisation's category, whether a particular tour or event requires an additional ticket, and the actual rate at checkout.

## Verified 12 November 2026 — 2027 registration rate model
- [NRF registration information](https://nrfbigshow.nrf.com/register): retail All-Access member per-person USD 1,100 / 1,200 / 1,300 / 1,400 for cutoff windows 2 Oct 2026 / 19 Nov 2026 / 7 Jan 2027 / from 8 Jan; non-member retail USD 2,100 / 2,200 / 2,300 / 2,400.
- Retail All-Access team purchase together from the same company: five–nine retail members USD 900, nonmembers USD 1,500; ten or more USD 750 and USD 1,250. This is an assumption-based cost model, not eligibility certification.
- Retail Expo through 30 Dec 2026 free; starting 31 Dec USD 100 for retail category (subject to verification).
- Non-retail members All-Access USD 2,600, Expo USD 2,000 through 30 Dec and USD 2,100 after; max five people/company. Non-retail nonmembers All-Access USD 3,800, Expo USD 3,200 through 30 Dec then USD 3,500; max two/company.
- [NRF eligibility guidance](https://nrfbigshow.nrf.com/register/registration-eligibility): company type, primary business, role, company-domain email and e-commerce verification affect the true category. Nonstandard journalist, student, analyst and educator tickets excluded from the rate calculator.
