# Topic 200 — Festival de Cannes 2027 evidence (checked 2026-11-10)

- [Official festival website](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/) states **11–22 May 2027**. Event date confirmed, not individual screening times.
- [Accreditation](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/take-part/accreditations/) describes professional, cinephile and 3 Days in Cannes categories but includes explicit 2026 dates, conditions and fees. Treat as historical context; verify 2027 requirements directly. Accreditation is not a guaranteed screening seat.
- [Online ticket office](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/take-part/your-festival-experience/online-ticket-office/) describes accreditation-dependent reservations and availability, but references 2026 opening. Do not copy 2026 timings into 2027.
- [2026 official catalogue](https://www.festival-cannes.com/catalogueofficiel/en/38-AUTOUR-DE-LA-MANIFESTATION-OTHER-EVENTS.html) notes general-public access under conditions for Cannes Cinéphiles. This is a counterexample to assuming all screenings are exclusive to industry badges; do not promise 2027 public access.
- [2027 regulations](https://cdn.festival-cannes.com/media/uploads/sites/2/2026/06/reglementsfc2027_en.pdf) refer to a specific short film industry programme and cannot be treated as a general spectator ticket schedule.

2027 programme, ticket inventory, categories, prices, deadlines, eligibility, transport and screening rules are not established by these sources. User-entered itineraries are hypothetical. Existing legacy #1–50 catalog and open PR search showed no Cannes duplicate as checked; recheck before merge because lanes can change.
